### Phase 6 certification campaign and stop decision

Phase 6 made the limits of the current audit architecture visible. From the first complete Golden attempt through the stopped audit, 21 complete-battery attempts consumed 546.5 minutes (9.11 hours) of recorded machine test time: five Golden runs and sixteen audit runs. Golden had two actionable product failures and then passed 142/142 three times in 29.4 to 30.6 minutes, but application corrections after those passes changed the source fingerprint. Audit had 15 failed certifications — ten harness assumptions and five product defects — followed by one deliberate operator cancellation. The cancellation was not a hidden failure: `2026-09-04T130118520Z-5e9bc2` had passed 47/149 with zero failures when it was stopped, classified `environment`, and its world was cleaned.

The asymmetry is structural. Golden is a compact, incrementally staged critical-path battery (25 specs, 6,782 lines, 142 tests, 180-second scenario budget). Audit is a retroactive exhaustive catalog (20 specs, 14,093 lines, 129 declared tests expanded to 149, 240-second scenario budget) whose serial consumers share one mutable world and often rely on earlier producers. UI semantics changed across the whole pass, so stale audit assumptions surfaced one at a time, late in runs. The product defects were real and retained; the repeated use of the complete audit as the discovery loop was the process failure. A green result under that process would not by itself establish repeatability.

The closure correction is bounded to this pass. Reconcile the complete UI impact map statically, run focused current-source proofs for the changed contracts, freeze the source, then authorize exactly one final local audit and Golden pair plus the unchanged DEV canary. A new failure stops the sequence for evidence-led diagnosis; it does not start another blind full-run loop. The separate pre-Wave-3 overhaul will preserve the Golden/Audit/Canary roles, audit flow IDs, business assertions, local/cloud split and three-tier enforcement rules while redesigning audit prerequisites, isolation/checkpoints, progress reporting and cumulative run budgets.

## Operating decision

The harness remains a serial shared-world integration suite because earlier slices intentionally feed later scenarios. It is not the iteration loop: focused local runs find defects, retained-world diagnostics inspect failures, and the complete local Audit and Golden batteries certify one final ordering. Only the cloud Canary and explicit milestone runs depend on live cloud-provider availability. Preflight cannot guarantee a later network window, so provider failures remain environment incidents and retries must not turn availability luck into acceptance evidence.

## Hardening acceptance record

The 2026-08-25 pass implements the nine retrospective actions:

1. Each run receives an immutable key and manifest; failure output, traces, screenshots, role state and world identifiers are copied into its own archive instead of being overwritten.
2. A failed DEV world is excluded from ordinary leftover sweeps and can be reopened by an explicitly focused diagnostic run.
3. Admin, Büro, employee and outsider storage states are recreated, organization-checked and exercised against a protected route before tests start; old or mismatched fixture state is refreshed.
4. The former single P1-16 Golden test is split into persisted setup, execution and boundary stages so a late role failure has a narrow target.
5. The runner enforces a full-rerun budget: classification and current-source focused proof precede the first retry, and two same-class failures block another attempt without a recorded override reason.
6. Iteration, diagnostic and certification are distinct lanes. Certification requires frozen source, a fresh build/server and a clean world.
7. Verbose Playwright output is written to `runner.log`; the interactive stream receives only start, final status and the first failure.
8. Preflight and unit policy checks enforce DEV routing and reachability, server ownership/freshness, retained-world cleanup, lane arguments, role-session age and the rerun rules. Serial browser batteries stop at their first failure.
9. This ledger records reusable causes and prevention. Future slice closure must link every failed certification to its run archive and focused proof.
