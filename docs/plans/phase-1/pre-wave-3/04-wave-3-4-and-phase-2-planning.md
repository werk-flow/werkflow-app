## Wave 3 slice by slice, against the market

The roadmap rows stay the bounded outcomes; this section adds, per slice, the current seam in the code, the competitor reference the slice answers, the concept-level additions, the decisions the protocol says must not be smuggled in, and a recommendation. Market facts come from the [competitive landscape](../../../product/competitive-landscape.md) and from the 2026-09-17 research (linked when the reports land). The pattern the landscape found holds across the wave: HERO, STREIT and pds are the three references for real stock, procurement and tools; plancraft, ToolTime and Craftnote sell catalogs and call them material. WerkFlow already has a real ledger; Wave 3 makes it complete.

### `P1-25` catalog and supplier master data

- Seam today: one supplier and one supplier article number per item, one purchase and one sale price, no price history, no pack or unit conversion, no successor, CSV import without preview. `inventory_import_batches` exists.
- Market: every serious suite imports DATANORM price files from the customer's wholesalers (HERO, ToolTime, plancraft Pro, STREIT, pds); HERO and STREIT show multiple supplier references and rebate groups; nobody in the modern group versions prices visibly.
- Adds: supplier references per item (preferred and alternatives, supplier article number, pack size, minimum order quantity, lead time, price validity), versioned cost and price with effective dates and snapshots referenced by later movements, unit and pack conversions with review, archive and successor links, an import workspace with row preview, duplicate resolution, reconciliation totals and a downloadable result, and a DATANORM 4 file import for article and price master data (the customer's own wholesaler files, an onboarding prerequisite, no partner contract).
- Do not smuggle: which DATANORM versions and rebate structures the first wholesalers need (fact from the owner's beta customer, Q3), the valuation price basis (Q5).
- Research facts that shape it (details in the research section): DATANORM is not an open standard, no TypeScript parser exists, wholesalers still ship version 4, the real customer price is list price minus the `.RAB` rebate group, and the files are CP850 with fixed-width and semicolon records. The parser is in-house work of one to two days from the public record layouts; the format documentation must not be republished.
- Recommendation: keep DATANORM 4 in `P1-25` as file import only (the same import workspace as CSV, with a fixed record map, rebate groups as first-class data); leave IDS, UGL, Open Masterdata and SHK Connect to `P1-34`. Split only if the import workspace alone exceeds a slice; then `P1-25a` (master data model) and `P1-25b` (import workspace with DATANORM).

### `P1-26` reservations and availability

- Seam today: `Verfügbar` is on hand minus open planned demand; planning creates demand only; readiness shows "nicht reserviert".
- Market: HERO advertises document-driven reservations and packing lists; STREIT and pds have commissioning; nobody publishes concurrent-reservation rules. WerkFlow's dispatch readiness and the `P1-24a` card chips are the visible consumers.
- Adds: a reservation record per material line (complete or partial, source location, holder, expiry), availability as on hand minus reservations with planned demand shown separately, shortage and coverage states per line and per job, release and reallocation with reasons, concurrency-safe allocation (row locks or an RPC), and the readiness chip values reserved, partial, missing.
- Do not smuggle: reservation policy (who may reserve, when a job may reserve, release on park or cancel), the exact `Verfügbar` definition, strict versus suggested preferred locations. Owner questions Q5 and Q6.
- Recommendation: `Verfügbar` = on hand minus reservations; planned demand is a separate column; manual reservation by `buero` and `admin` only; parking or cancelling a job releases reservations with a visible event; no automatic reservation from a status change (capability-map rule 3).

### `P1-27` picking, consumption, return and billability

- Seam today: `job_take` and `job_return` movements from the office and the field work pack; billable quantity per line without a review flow; no consumption state distinct from taken.
- Market: HERO's job consumption and QR scans, STREIT `Lager+`, pds material app; the field flow "identify, confirm, location, quantity, save" is the standard. Billability review exists only in the incumbents.
- Adds: pick from a reservation, consumed and installed as a state distinct from taken, scrap and damage, correction with original and corrected values, over-return guard with a manager exception, cost snapshot per movement (from `P1-25` price versions), billable review in the office with reasons (warranty, goodwill, rework, waste, customer-supplied), the handoff record that Wave 4's billable review reads. The immutability trigger on both inventory ledgers lands here.
- Gate: `GG-08`.
- Recommendation: keep the field flow at four steps; every commercial decision stays in the office. This is the slice where the `P1-16` work pack gains its final material section.

### `P1-28` transfers and vehicle stock

- Seam today: `transfer_in` and `transfer_out` exist as movement kinds without a flow; `vehicle` is a location type.
- Market: STREIT and pds do vehicle stock and transfers; HERO's "warehouse movement book"; plancraft and ToolTime have nothing here.
- Adds: a transfer record with source, destination, responsible person, quantities and status; immediate transfers for simple cases; dispatched, in transit, received for vehicles and remote stores; partial receipt, discrepancy, cancellation and loss; in-transit quantity visible at neither end; the `P1-24a` row kind `vehicle` becomes real here if the owner wants vehicle rows on the board in Wave 3 (Q6).
- Recommendation: a vehicle stays a location; a vehicle row on the Plantafel shows the vehicle's assignments per day from a small vehicle-assignment record owned by planning, not by inventory. Decide in Q6 whether that record is in `P1-28` or deferred with the fleet gate.

### `P1-29` demand consolidation and purchase orders

- Seam today: nothing; global minimum and target stock exist per item.
- Market: STREIT and pds have purchase suggestions and supplier comparison; HERO has purchase orders; the cloud peers hand a cart to IDS at most.
- Adds: a demand worklist from shortages, reorder levels, uncovered reservations and manual requests; supplier comparison on price, pack, lead time and preferred supplier; a purchase request with role-based approval through the `P1-07` attention pattern; purchase orders with revisions, expected dates, confirmations, backorders and cancellation; duplicate-order prevention; a PO document (PDF) the office sends itself.
- Do not smuggle: approval thresholds and roles (Q5), the delivery channel (Q9).
- Recommendation: file-first. The PO is a document in the library linked to the order; sending it is the office's mailbox until `P1-46` or the IDS handoff in `P1-34`.

### `P1-30` goods receipt and supplier returns

- Adds: receipt against an order or without one, accepted, damaged, short, excess, substituted and rejected quantities, receipt into a chosen location or directly to a job with ownership preserved, delivery-note linkage (document), supplier return with expected credit, and the matching record that `P1-41` reads for incoming-bill matching.
- Gate: `GG-09`.
- Market: standard in the incumbents; ToolTime's and plancraft's delivery-note OCR (STREIT v27 too) is Phase 2 territory here, the deterministic part (a receipt line list) is this slice.

### `P1-31` counts, corrections and valuation

- Adds: full, cycle, location and spot counts; blind counts; assignment, pause, second count; discrepancy review before correction; movement policy for locations under count; a signed completion record; operational valuation at the chosen cost basis; slow-moving, negative-stock and variance views; the snapshot that keeps historical job costs stable.
- Do not smuggle: the valuation method (Q5). Recommendation: moving average per item and location as the operational basis, labelled as operational, never as an accounting ledger.
- German context: §240 HGB requires every `Kaufmann` to draw up an `Inventar` at each fiscal year end; §241 allows a `permanente Inventur` without a count on the balance date only when a gapless stock ledger plus at least one physical count per article and year secures the figures, and a `verlegte Inventur` within three months before to two months after; §241a exempts sole traders under 800,000 € revenue and 80,000 € profit, never a GmbH. The `Steuerberater` expects signed count lists, the valuation basis, a description of the procedure and a difference analysis, retained ten years. That is the sales argument for this slice: a gapless ledger and a signed count record make the permanent inventory legal for a GmbH. The product claim stays "supports the count", never "GoBD-compliant".

### `P1-32` tools and individually tracked assets

- Seam today: `inventory_asset_instances` with asset tag and serial; no custody, condition, inspection or lifecycle.
- Market: HERO tools, STREIT `Werkzeug+`, pds `pds Werkzeug` (poorly rated), Craftnote and HERO schedule tools on the board. The German driver is DGUV Vorschrift 3 inspection of electrical equipment and the ladder and PPE checks: "Prüfung fällig" is a real office task, not a nice-to-have.
- Adds: instance lifecycle states (available, in use, reserved, maintenance, inspection due, damaged, lost, retired, disposed), custody handover and return with chain of custody, job allocation, condition and issue reporting with a block rule for unsafe equipment, inspection and maintenance history with due dates, documents and photos, QR identification, the readiness chip value for tools, and the `tool` row kind on the Plantafel.
- Recommendation: tools are instances of catalog items; a vehicle gets an optional asset instance for inspection and custody only if Q6 says so.

### `P1-33` offboarding closure with asset return

- Adds: the last people-lifecycle piece: outstanding time and leave, reassignment of work and approvals, access revocation, return of tools, vehicles and stock in custody, exports, and retained (or explicitly anonymized) historical identity. It must replace the contained defect `A1-07` (members with time history can only leave through employment transitions since SI-006).

### `P1-34` barcode, QR and wholesaler standards

- Seam today: multiple barcodes per item with typed values; no scanning; no labels.
- Adds: browser scanning through the native `BarcodeDetector` API with a JavaScript fallback library, one shared resolve step that maps a code to an item, location, transfer, order, delivery or asset with collision review, scan parity with manual search (same domain actions), printable labels and replacement labels, and the standards acceptance: DATANORM (already imported in `P1-25`, here the versions and rebate structures are pinned), IDS Connect cart round trip, UGL document exchange, Open Masterdata enrichment, SHK Connect only for the services the partners actually run. Every standard gets partner, version, direction, objects, entitlement, setup owner, timing, error recovery and whether a supplier contract is needed.
- Gate: `GG-10`.
- Do not smuggle: which standards and partners have demand (Q3), scanner hardware and formats.
- Research facts that shape it: IDS Connect 2.5 is a `multipart/form-data` POST to the shop with a callback URL and two public XSDs, no certification, and gives live price and availability through `artikelSuche` and the deep link; the shop URLs come from ITEK's Open Connect directory, free for craft-software vendors. UGL 5.0 is a public fixed-width file format for `Bestellung`, `Auftragsbestätigung` and `Lieferschein`, moved by FTP or shop upload with wholesaler-specific naming; it is the only path today for an automatic goods receipt from a delivery note. Open Masterdata's documentation is behind a paywall and needs a wholesaler-issued key per customer. Safari on iOS ships `BarcodeDetector` disabled, so the WASM fallback is mandatory for field phones.
- Recommendation: browser-first scanning now (`BarcodeDetector` plus the `barcode-detector` polyfill on `zxing-wasm`; labels with `bwip-js` into the PDF engine), native scanning in `P1-49` with VisionCamera; standards in the order DATANORM (done in `P1-25`), IDS Connect 2.5 (cart out, order back, live price lookup), UGL (order out, confirmation and delivery note in, if a beta wholesaler uses it), Open Masterdata (single-article refresh, after IDS), SHK Connect and Open Connect only as the directory that finds shop endpoints. Watch ODX as the JSON successor of UGL; do not build on it yet.

## Wave 4 slice by slice, against the market

Wave 4 is the risk concentration (protocol caution 2). The product baseline is empty: no catalog positions, no offer, no invoice. The market reference is the whole field: every competitor in the landscape ships offers and invoices, e-invoices, and DATEV; the incumbents add GAEB, Aufmaß by REB/VOB, §13b, Sicherheitseinbehalt and integrated accounting. The 2026-09-17 research puts the bar precisely: plancraft, openHandwerk, TAIFUN and pds claim alternative and demand positions, Aufmaß tied to positions, cumulative `Abschlagsrechnungen` with a netting `Schlussrechnung`, `Sicherheitseinbehalt` net or gross, §13b, `Bauabzugsteuer`, Skonto, multi-level dunning and post-calculation; HERO and ToolTime are weaker on the construction-specific items and lean on embedded payments instead. WerkFlow's differentiator is not more invoice types; it is that billing reads the operational record (approved time from `P1-23`, net consumption from `P1-27`, Aufmaß and Regie evidence from `P1-15`, the handover from `P1-17`) instead of a retyped list, and that it matches the first group on correctness before adding anything else.

The legal clock: since 2025-01-01 every business must be able to receive e-invoices; issuers above 800,000 € turnover must issue them from 2027-01-01, everyone from 2028-01-01; consumers still get PDF. XRechnung 3.0.2 is in force until at least mid-2027 with 4.0 expected in spring 2027 on EN 16931-1:2026; ZUGFeRD 2.5.2 is valid since 2026-09-01 and the EN 16931 profile is the one to produce. One invoice model must therefore emit PDF, ZUGFeRD PDF/A-3 and XRechnung (UBL first) with the EN 16931 mapping in one module.

### `P1-35` commercial catalog, rate cards and calculation

- Adds: commercial positions (labor types with rates, travel and vehicle charges, services, materials with an optional link to an inventory item, subcontracted work, fees, surcharges, discounts, text positions), price lists by customer group or customer, effective-dated and versioned prices, calculation inputs kept distinct (supplier price, labor cost, overhead, risk, markup, discount, target margin), net and gross with transparent rounding, approval thresholds for margin and discount, import with preview.
- Market: plancraft's calculation and post-calculation in Pro, HERO's calculation and margin, STREIT's `Projektkalkulation`; the German trade convention is `Kalkulation` with `Lohn`, `Material`, `Geräte`, `Fremdleistung` and `Zuschläge`, which the position model must express without forcing every small business through it.
- Do not smuggle: calculation defaults (Q4 asks the owner which scenario defines them).

### `P1-36` offers

- Adds: offer states (draft, internal review, ready, sent, accepted, partly accepted, rejected, expired, superseded, cancelled), sections, optional and alternative positions, text blocks and templates with branding, internal cost and margin never on the customer document, a deterministic PDF, delivery by download and email evidence, acceptance evidence recorded with identity, time and version (uploaded signed offer or recorded confirmation), supersession, conversion into a job, project, material demand or deposit request.
- Market: table stakes everywhere; plancraft's AI quote draft and HERO Command are Phase 2 for WerkFlow. Online acceptance links (ToolTime) are an external surface and stay a portal decision.
- Engine: the PDF engine decided in Q7. The handover already renders deterministic HTML in `P1-17`; Gotenberg (Chromium plus PDF/A-3b plus a Factur-X embedding route in one container) keeps offer and invoice layouts in the HTML and CSS the team already writes, so `P1-36` should be built on the engine `P1-40` needs rather than on a throwaway renderer. Typst 0.15 with `attach` is the alternative if the team prefers a typesetting language.

### `P1-37` order confirmation, contract baseline and change orders

- Adds: the immutable baseline from the accepted offer version, change orders (`Nachtrag`) with reason, scope and price delta, schedule effect and customer evidence, the cumulative view, warnings on unapproved extra work, and the deliberate operational handoff into planning and material demand. `P1-15`'s Regie- und Änderungsnachweis is the field evidence; this slice gives it a price.
- Gate: `GG-11`.

### `P1-38` billable-work review

- Adds: the worklist that joins contract positions, approved changes, measured quantities (`P1-15` Aufmaß revisions), approved time and travel (`P1-23`), net billable material (`P1-27`), receipts and external cost (`P1-30`), previous billing and exceptions into a reviewed billable set; accepting creates an invoice draft, never an issued invoice.
- This is the slice that makes Wave 4 different from the competitors' "type the invoice again" flows; it is also the one with the most cross-domain reads and needs its own measured scenario.

### `P1-39` customer invoices and credits

- Adds: standard, deposit (`Abschlag`), partial (`Teil`), cumulative and final (`Schluss`) invoices, credits and corrections linked to the original, organization number ranges with uniqueness and controlled issuance (gaps and several ranges per year or branch are allowed, cancelled numbers documented), immutable issued content, the §14 Abs. 4 UStG fields, performance period, payment terms, Skonto (in XRechnung as structured `#SKONTO#` text in BT-20), `Sicherheitseinbehalt` where a contract has it, §13b reverse charge for `Bauleistungen` with the exact wording and tax category AE, the `Bauabzugsteuer` reference to the `Freistellungsbescheinigung` (the customer withholds 15 %, so the open item must expect a short payment that is not a dunning case), delivery evidence, states from draft to paid, disputed, credited and written off. Retention for invoices is eight years for those issued from 2025.
- Do not smuggle: everything in caution 2. `decision_blocked` until the expert review is commissioned (Q4). The review's list from the research: VAT treatment of cumulative `Abschlagsrechnungen` and the netting `Schlussrechnung`, the `Sicherheitseinbehalt` tax point, §13b customer classification with USt 1 TG, `Bauabzugsteuer` wording and certificate checks, Skonto as text versus allowance, number ranges and `Storno` versus `Korrektur`, SKR03 and SKR04 mapping in the export, the GoBD procedure documentation for immutable invoices; and for a lawyer: dunning fees and default interest under §288 BGB, payment-term AGB, the DATEV and bank-provider contracts.
- Retention: the issued PDF and XML land on R2 under immutable keys with the archive state from Q8.

### `P1-40` XRechnung and ZUGFeRD

- Adds: generation and validation of XRechnung 3.0.2 (UBL first, CII second) and ZUGFeRD 2.5.2 EN 16931, import of structured supplier invoices, readable previews of structured fields, exact original and generated files with the validation result stored as evidence, and fallback delivery. Peppol transport stays a decision gate: no B2B mandate exists, and a managed access point costs roughly 0,10 to 0,25 € per invoice when a customer needs it.
- Engine: generation in TypeScript (`@e-invoice-eu/core`, the only TypeScript option with UBL, CII and every profile, under a WTFPL licence that legal review may dislike; or `@stackforge-eu/factur-x`, CII only, EUPL, freshest); `node-zugferd` is a stale beta. Validation is a gate, not a feature: the KoSIT validator in daemon mode with the current configuration release plus Mustang's validator for the PDF/A layer, in a small Java container on Railway. No invoice reaches "Versendet" without a green validator report.
- Market: table stakes since the 2025 receive obligation.
- Gate: `GG-12`.

### `P1-41` incoming bills and expenses

- Adds: capture by upload and structured e-invoice import (deterministic XRechnung and ZUGFeRD parsing, no OCR yet), duplicate detection, matching to order, receipt, delivery note, job and cost category, variance visibility, split allocation, approval by amount and role through the attention pattern, dispute, credit expected and received, employee expenses with receipts and reimbursement state, ready-for-accounting state. OCR for paper and photo bills is `P1-44` (worker) and Phase 2 (extraction).

### `P1-42` open items, bank matching and dunning

- Adds: customer and supplier open items with aging, bank statement import by file (camt.053 version 08, which German banks have moved to since November 2025, MT940 as legacy, and per-bank CSV maps) with idempotent import keyed on the bank transaction id, match proposals on reference, amount with Skonto tolerance and IBAN, one-to-many and split allocation, reversible unmatch, an expected-short-payment rule for `Bauabzugsteuer`, dunning levels with grace, fees only where validated, preview before sending, and the worklists. A live bank connection is a decision gate with a monthly cost: finAPI (BaFin AISP, FinTS fallback, from 100 € per month) is the pragmatic first provider; GoCardless Bank Account Data has closed new sign-ups; consent renews every 180 days under the 2023 delegated regulation.
- Market: HERO Wallet (on Swan, virtual IBAN per customer) and ToolTime Pay (on Adyen, 1 % capped at 10 € for transfers) show the embedded-payments shape; plancraft shows dashboards. WerkFlow starts with matching and never builds a wallet; a payment link through Stripe or Mollie is a later option once open items and dunning are stable.

### `P1-43` post-calculation and accountant-ready export

- Adds: source-linked job and project post-calculation (planned versus actual revenue, labor, material, external cost, variance explanations, completeness warnings, forecast at completion labelled as an estimate), and the period export with account, tax key, debtor and creditor mappings, control totals, document links, rejection and re-export without duplicates. The first export is the DATEV-Format `EXTF` Buchungsstapel file plus a documents ZIP (no partner program needed, the `Steuerberater` imports it, Agenda accepts the same file); Lexware Office and sevDesk APIs second because customers ask; the DATEV Rechnungsdatenservice or Buchungsdatenservice third, as a `P1-50` connector, since Marktplatz partner status needs a Datenservice in use by 25 customers with three references and carries a monthly fee plus a click price.
- Gate: `GG-13`.

`P1-35` to `P1-38` can be built and accepted on internal evidence. `P1-39` to `P1-43` need the expert review; the sensible order is to commission the review during `P1-35` so its findings shape the invoice model before `P1-39` starts, not after. ### Guardrails: given by the stack versus built

Given: organization scoping and RLS, the attention pattern for approvals, the eleven guarded ledgers, signed URLs with the organization prefix (step 5 tightening), the background-read registry as the pattern for read-only tool access, Postgres transactions for idempotent actions.

Built in Phase 2 (or `P1-53`): an automation run ledger (trigger, inputs read with timestamps, proposal, approval, action, result, cost), a per-organization budget with hard stops, an execution identity distinct from the user who configured the workflow, a tool allow-list per template and per agent, idempotency keys for every external action, retry and terminal failure states, a kill switch per organization and per workflow, prompt and output retention rules, PII minimization before provider calls (pseudonymized employee identifiers, no personnel documents), an evaluation set of real permission-safe SHK documents, and cost display in the owner's language.

### First slice candidates

1. `Arbeitsbericht` from a voice note: the field worker records, the model drafts the report into a new `P1-15` revision in draft state, the worker edits and saves. Needs a speech provider and the model. Highest field value, lowest data risk, everything else exists.
2. `Anfrage` from an email: an inbound address per organization, the model extracts customer, site, urgency and summary into a request draft with the original attached. Needs inbound email. Highest office value.
3. Daily brief: one German summary per office user and per field worker from the plan, dispatches, blockers and open approvals. Needs nothing external; delivery inside `/aufgaben`.

Recommendation: 1 then 3, both as Level 1 assist with no external action, as a bounded pilot lane if the owner opens one (Q1); 2 waits for the inbound-email decision. #### Why the beta customer's facts matter (answer to Q3)

The owner is right that the product cannot be built around one customer: every SHK business will have a different wholesaler mix, accounting tool and legal form, and the slices must serve all of them. The facts matter for three narrower reasons, none of which changes the product scope:

- Order and fixtures. `P1-25` needs real DATANORM files from a real wholesaler to test against, and `P1-34` needs one shop that answers IDS Connect requests. Which wholesaler comes first is decided by whoever WerkFlow can get files and a test account from; the beta customer is the cheapest source. The parser serves every wholesaler once it works for one.
- Migration. `P1-47` migrates a customer's data in. Knowing that Willert runs Sykasoft turns "assisted migration" into a concrete export-import path (Sykasoft's customer, article and open-document exports); the research below profiles what Sykasoft can export. Every later customer arrives from some other tool, and Sykasoft is one of the SHK-specific incumbents, so this is the first of a small set of migration sources, not a one-off.
- Acceptance. The `GG-10` and `GG-13` journeys and the Wave 4 expert review are more honest when they run against the real accountant's expectations (DATEV or not, GmbH or not) than against a guess. The product still ships both paths; the review just starts from a real case.

So the questions stay on the list for the owner to ask Willert when convenient, and the answers set fixtures and order, never scope.
