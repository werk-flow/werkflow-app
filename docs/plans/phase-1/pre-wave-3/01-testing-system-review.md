## Why this step exists

The Step 3 release campaign needed eleven runs over 25 hours. Seven of the ten failures were real defects and were repaired; the campaign's cost came from the system itself, and the owner had to intervene to stop repeated full runs. The exact account is in the [Step 3 closing account](../hardening-2026-09/07-step-3-final-beta-acceptance.md#closing-account). The owner's requirement, verbatim in intent: agents must question whether the way they test makes sense and improve the system when it does not, without the owner pointing it out; documenting what went wrong is not evolving.

## Findings to act on, with evidence

1. **The environment digest rotates with WSL.** `calculateBuildInputs` in `lib/testing/build-identity.ts` hashes the whole `.env.local`, which carries the local stack address (`http://172.19.x.x:54321`). WSL changed that address overnight (172.19.150.82 during the runs, 172.19.155.11 afterwards) and every local proof became unreusable. The build receipt genuinely needs the raw address, because the client bundle embeds `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`; the proof snapshot does not. Separate the two: a proof environment identity keyed on backend identity (local stack, DEV or PROD project ref, bucket name) rather than the raw file. Tier 1 by construction, with a unit test that rotates the address and expects the same proof digest.
2. **No circuit breaker.** The campaign budget record (`campaign-extend`, `campaign-close`) counts certification-lane attempts only; release runs are group-lane, so eight full attempts triggered nothing. Add a breaker in `scripts/verify.ts`: a second full release attempt after a failed one is refused unless a focused run of every failed group has passed on the current inputs or the application source changed. Tier 2, with a unit test on the planning function.
3. **Incident entries must carry a tier.** Decision 0005 asks for it; nothing checks it. Extend `docs:check` (`scripts/check-docs.ts`) so every incident entry names Tier 1, Tier 2 or an explicit "Tier 3 because …". Tier 2.
4. **Retention.** `.agent-logs` reached 25 GB before a one-time prune on 14 September deleted 22.2 GB of traces from 1,213 runs while keeping every manifest, report and latency archive and the artifacts of the 37 runs current proofs cite. Make that a `test:runs prune` command with the same keep rules and a size guard in the runner. Tier 2.
5. **Comment-only and path-only changes invalidate proofs.** Content hashing is right for application code, but a changed comment in `lib/storage/r2.ts` invalidated all 40 groups on 14 September. Decide whether the proof snapshot may hash source with comments stripped (a compiler-based transform, not a regex), or whether this stays as is. Record the decision either way.
6. **Freshness judged by reference, not by a fixed line.** The tolerance is a band around a fixed 2000 ms target. The performance scenarios compare a median of three samples with a reviewed reference. Evaluate giving freshness stages the same model; the eight Golden samples of the last run ranged 1055 to 1689 ms.
7. **Queued Server Action reads.** One client's Server Actions run one after another. Traces of runs 5 and 10 show live-view read bursts (attention counts, clock state, time entries, targets, lifecycle) delaying the next read or submit by 0.8 to 2.6 s. This is product work already in the [enforcement backlog](../../../technical/enforcement-ladder-backlog.md); decide with the owner whether it belongs here or in a product slice.
8. **Stale path pointers in immutable inputs.** Comments in `lib/storage/r2.ts`, `lib/supabase/fetch-with-timeout.ts`, `tests/golden/support/date-ownership.ts`, `tests/canary/canary.spec.ts`, `tests/audit/README.md`, both Playwright configs and `supabase/config.toml` still name plan files at their pre-14-September paths under `docs/plans/`; they were left unchanged to keep proofs valid. Fix them in the same change that first invalidates those inputs anyway.
9. **The campaign record.** `campaign-close` refuses the record `0386e1c0-0966-4e20-8048-4615e8d86b55` because certification-lane runs from 5 September are its latest per suite. Decide whether the certification lane still exists under decision 0007 and retire the record or the lane.
10. **Catch-up bursts on focus.** The Realtime provider (`components/realtime/realtime-provider.tsx`) re-reads every live view 50 ms after a window focus or visibility change and on the database-ready signal. Playwright's page switches between the acting and receiving sessions fire those focus events, so every switch costs a burst of five to eight serialized reads (PERF-03 trace, 13 September: a re-read 85 ms after the held read was aborted; P1-24 trace, 14 September: three bursts within two seconds of a page load). A minimum blur duration before a focus catch-up would remove most of it; decide with the product angle (a user switching windows for five seconds needs no full re-read while the socket is alive).
11. **Baseline transfer chain.** The eleven performance references in `lib/testing/performance-baselines.json` were transferred context-only five times during Step 3 (scripts and snapshots under `.agent-logs/step3-repairs/`). A reviewed recalibration (three clean samples per scenario on the current candidate) should retire the chain; the measurement digest should also stop covering helpers that no measured scenario calls (`expectLiveWithin`, `expectReadyWithin`, `holdNextCalendarRead`), which is what forced the transfers.
12. **Run manifests record `projectRef: "172"`** for local runs, the first octet of the WSL address rather than a backend identity; the same root cause as finding 1 and the same fix.
13. **Parallelism.** `--jobs 2` caps browser groups at two and keeps freshness, performance, SQL and Golden exclusive for measurement integrity. Worlds isolate data; the cap protects the one recorded server and the host. Measure whether three or four jobs keep the freshness samples inside the tolerance before raising it. (Superseded 2026-09-25: the cap is eight, the default one, and the measurement is in testing.md, "Workers".)

## How the system must evolve on its own

Write the rule into the testing protocol and make it mechanical where possible: after any failed verification run, the next action is a focused run of the failed group; after two consecutive failed campaign runs, the agent writes a harness hypothesis into the incident log before running anything; every incident entry ends in a Tier 1 or Tier 2 change or a stated reason. The breaker in finding 2 makes the first two rules impossible to skip.

### Decisions

The owner took every decision below on 2026-09-14 after reading this record; the receiving plan files carry the work.

- D1 (owner: keep): comment-insensitive hashing (A5) stays. Reverting would be one function in `source-content.ts`.
- D2 (agent, owner informed): freshness stays a fixed two-second contract with the 25%/250 ms tolerance. The per-stage single-sample model cannot be turned into a median without changing the business journey or mixing builds. The verification report already keeps every sample; a trend view over reports is possible later without changing the verdict rule.
- D3 (owner: fix in the UX step): the queued Server Action reads (finding 7) are product work, scheduled in [step 3](03-clock-fab-and-calendar-ux.md#owner-decisions-already-taken); the freshness helper's acting-page idle wait keeps the measurement honest until then.
- D4 (owner: retire): the certification lane (`test:golden`, `test:audit`, `test:canary`, `campaign-extend`, `campaign-close`, the rerun-grant budget) no longer takes part in acceptance under decision 0007; the release breaker replaces its budget for the group lane. [Step 2](02-code-quality-regression-proofing.md#owner-decisions-already-taken) removes the lane and its commands; the record `0386e1c0` stays open as history.
- D5 (owner: adopt): a minimum hidden duration before a focus catch-up in the Realtime provider (30 seconds while the socket reports subscribed, immediate after a reconnect), scheduled in [step 3](03-clock-fab-and-calendar-ux.md#owner-decisions-already-taken) with a component contract.
- D6 (owner: adopt): recalibrate the eleven references from three clean samples per scenario on the beta-acceptance candidate and delete the transferred copies; the procedure lives in [step 5](05-beta-acceptance-and-production-rollout.md#performance-reference-recalibration). This step transferred twice more and activated no new numbers.
- D7 (owner: keep): `--jobs 2` stays. Measured groups are exclusive anyway, so a higher cap changes only untimed audit groups and host load; the run-8 memory incident argues against raising it before the host has more headroom. Superseded 2026-09-25 by the [execution-model amendment of decision 0007](../../../decisions/0007-independent-test-groups.md#amendment-2026-09-25-the-execution-model).
- Prune (owner: keep while useful): `bun run test:runs prune` is not executed. Among the 380 prunable runs are the eighth release run's A3-11 and P1-20 traces (`2026-09-13T213332683Z-8c28da`, `2026-09-13T220317968Z-7405f8`), the only evidence of the still unexplained early-closed action responses, and every classified campaign failure. The guard at 10 GB decides when pruning becomes necessary; until then the traces stay.
