# Inventory

Inventory (`Lager`) is the operational record of SHK materials, consumables, tools, assets, locations, stock movements and job material. It tells the business what is on hand and where, what upcoming work needs, and what employees took and returned. A field worker must understand it, and the office gets an auditable material flow.

## Current Product Baseline

Admin and Büro get an organization-scoped catalog, their own locations, manual stock movements, CSV import and material planning on jobs and projects. Assigned field workers take and return material on their jobs.

- **Catalog.** Item types are material, consumable, tool and asset. An item carries several barcodes. The barcode entered on the item becomes its primary barcode, and the previous primary stays attached. A barcode that belongs to another item refuses the save. Saving an item, its barcode and its first stock count is all or nothing. Minimum and target stock are set once per item and apply across all locations. The web app has no camera scanning.
- **Central inventory.** Only Admin and Büro open `/inventar`, with views for all items, Lager, planned material and movements. Search, filters and summary counts cover the whole organization, not only the visible page. The movements view shows only the most recent movements, without paging or search.
- **Availability.** The overview shows stock by item and by location, open planned quantity and `Verfügbar`. `Verfügbar` is total stock minus open planned demand. It is not a reservation. When planned demand exceeds the stock of an item with tracked stock, `Verfügbar` reads „Fehlmenge“ with the missing quantity, and the item takes the status `Unterdeckt`, which outranks `Leer` and `Knapp`. The summary counts these items, and the status filter finds them. An item without tracked stock never shows a shortfall: its `Verfügbar` stops at 0.
- **Stock movements.** Only managers record manual additions and removals at a location. No correction flow exists: a new addition or removal fixes a wrong booking. Stock cannot go below zero. Every movement records who, when, where, why, and the quantity before and after. A movement from job material also keeps its job or project. Users have no transfer flow.
- **Locations and categories.** Managers create their own locations, labeled Lager, room, shelf, vehicle or other. WerkFlow creates no default warehouse, so the inventory mirrors the real rooms, shelves and vehicles. Each organization starts with editable SHK categories. Category names carry no product logic.
- **Units.** Every unit accepts a decimal quantity.
- **CSV import.** The initial inventory at onboarding goes through CSV import with column mapping. A file with more data rows or columns than one import takes is refused when it is chosen, and the file field names the limit. The browser reads the file; only its rows and a hash of its content reach the server. When a file with the same content was booked before, the dialog names the date and the person and waits until the user continues or picks another file.
- **Import preview.** After the mapping, the preview lists every row with its effect, and nothing is booked before „Jetzt buchen“. The preview and the booking run the same database rule, so they agree on create or match. Matching checks the internal SKU first, then a barcode, also against articles an earlier row of the same file creates. A row shows whether it creates or matches an article, the unit as understood, the quantity per Lager, a Lager the import creates, and the resulting stock. Totals above the rows count new, matched and rejected rows and the stock each Lager receives per unit. A matched row adds its quantity to the article's stock as an `Eingang` and leaves the article as it is: no field, barcode, category or supplier changes. A new article's quantity is its first count, and the import creates a Lager only for a quantity it books.
- **Import refusals.** A row without a name, with an empty unit or with a unit outside the unit list is rejected and never becomes Stück. A matched row whose unit differs from the article's unit is rejected („Einheit weicht ab: Stück statt Meter“); the import converts no unit. A row whose barcode belongs to another article is rejected. A rejected row leaves nothing behind, not even its category, supplier or Lager. A row with a quantity but no Lager keeps its article, books no quantity, and counts separately.
- **Stammdaten only.** „Nur Stammdaten und Preise“ creates articles with their prices and books no stock and creates no Lager, whatever the quantity column holds. The preview says so.
- **Import booking.** The booking books the previewed rows of its batch once: a confirm sent twice books once and answers the first result. The result lists every rejected row and every row without a Lager with its reason, and offers them as a CSV report built in the browser, with the original cells and the file's line numbers. A booking with no such row closes the dialog with the counts.
- **Job and project material.** Managers plan catalog items on jobs and projects without changing stock. Managers and assigned employees take items from a location or return them. A take or return changes stock immediately, with no approval step. An employee may take an existing item that was never planned, and the line is marked unplanned. A refused take creates no unplanned line. A take on a line whose item a manager changed after it was opened is refused with "Die Materialposition wurde inzwischen geändert" and books nothing. Projects show direct material, material from child jobs and the total separately. Lines keep planned, taken, returned and billable quantities apart. Nothing commercial uses the billable quantity. An employee reads neither the billable quantity nor the billing flag, not even through the Data API.
- **Field material actions.** An assigned employee works with the job's material inside the field work pack. A take started on a planned line opens on that line alone; the catalog search opens only with „Weiteres Material“. A line shows taken and returned quantities as plain facts („2 Stück entnommen“), and what stays at the job once something came back. The unplanned-item search hides supplier, price, valuation and billability, and its hint names no supplier. Employees cannot create items, open `/inventar` or use project-level material.
- **Read-only consumers.** Templates, dispatch readiness, the work lifecycle, handover, service cases and maintenance plans show material facts and never create, reserve, consume or return stock. Readiness labels planned material as not reserved and tools as not assessed.
- **Tools and assets.** Tools and assets are catalog items. No screen creates, tracks or assigns an individual instance.

### Current limits

- Not built: reservation, picking, approval, transfer, purchase order, goods receipt, supplier return, reorder worklist, formal stock count, valuation report, wholesale-standard integration, Excel import.
- The CSV import converts no unit, merges no duplicate articles and changes no data of a matched article.
- No tool checkout, custody, maintenance, inspection, loss or retirement workflow exists.
