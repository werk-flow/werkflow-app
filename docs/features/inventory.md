# Inventory

Inventory (`Lager`) is the operational record of SHK materials, consumables, tools, assets, locations, stock movements and job material. It tells the business what is on hand and where, what upcoming work needs, and what employees took and returned. A field worker must understand it, and the office gets an auditable material flow.

## Current Product Baseline

Admin and Büro get an organization-scoped catalog, their own locations, manual stock movements, CSV import and material planning on jobs and projects. Assigned field workers take, use and return material on their jobs.

- **Catalog.** Item types are material, consumable, tool and asset. An item carries several barcodes. The barcode entered on the item becomes its primary barcode, and the previous primary stays attached. A barcode that belongs to another item refuses the save. Saving an item, its barcode and its first stock count is all or nothing. Minimum and target stock are set once per item and apply across all locations. The web app has no camera scanning.
- **Suppliers.** An article has any number of supplier references: the supplier, its article number, and the purchase unit with its conversion to the base unit, entered as two numbers and read back as a sentence before saving („1 Rolle = 50 Meter“). A conversion of zero or less is refused. A supplier article number belongs to one article of the organization. An article with references has exactly one preferred reference: the first one, or the one marked preferred; removing it prefers the oldest remaining one. The preferred reference is the article's supplier everywhere else. An article number that an older article carries without a supplier is offered for its first reference. No supplier contact person, minimum order, lead time or rebate is kept.
- **Prices.** An article's purchase price and list sale price are dated: an amount in cents per 1, 10, 100 or 1000 base units, valid from a day, with its source (Von Hand, CSV-Import, DATANORM, or „Übernommen“ for the single price an article carried before) and the person who recorded it. A recorded price is never changed or deleted; a new price corrects it, also for the same day, and may start in the past or the future. The price of a day is, per kind, the one with the latest first day up to that day, and among prices of the same first day the latest recorded. Each price keeps the base unit it was stated in: after a unit change, earlier prices stay as history and apply only while the article has their unit, so a change back makes them count again. The unit of an article that a supplier sells in packs cannot change until those pack sizes are removed. One database rule answers it for the price reader, the article dialog and the cost of recorded use. An article with price history is archived, never deleted.
- **Article dialog.** A new article starts with one supplier and its purchase and sale price per base unit from today. A saved article opens on „Stammdaten“, with „Lieferanten“ and „Preise“ beside it; each change in those tabs saves at once and the banner confirms it. „Preise“ shows today's purchase and sale price with first day and source, every recorded price with „Gilt heute“, „Kommt“ or „Früher“, and the form for a new price. A failed read of suppliers or prices shows „Erneut laden“ in its tab. Employees never open the dialog and read neither suppliers nor prices, not even through the Data API.
- **Central inventory.** Only Admin and Büro open `/inventar`, with views for all items, Lager, planned material and movements. Search, filters and summary counts cover the whole organization, not only the visible page.
- **Movement history.** The movements view holds every stock movement and every use of job material, newest first, 50 to a page, with the total of all matches. It searches the article by name, SKU, manufacturer, supplier article number and barcode, and filters by Lager, kind, person and a date range of Berlin days. A Lager filter leaves out uses, which happen on the job. A use („Verbraucht“) and its correction („Verbrauch korrigiert“) are kinds of their own: they show no Lager, no quantity before or after, and „Bestand bleibt gleich“, and never a cost. Each row names the person who booked it, and a movement or use of job material links to its job or project. The kind filter offers the kinds the app books; transfers and the stock kind „Korrektur“ have no flow yet and show only under „Alle Bewegungen“. A failed read shows „Erneut laden“ in the view while the rest of the page stays usable, and a search without matches offers „Filter zurücksetzen“.
- **Availability.** The overview shows stock by item and by location, open planned quantity and `Verfügbar`. `Verfügbar` is total stock minus open planned demand. It is not a reservation. When planned demand exceeds the stock of an item with tracked stock, `Verfügbar` reads „Fehlmenge“ with the missing quantity, and the item takes the status `Unterdeckt`, which outranks `Leer` and `Knapp`. The summary counts these items, and the status filter finds them. An item without tracked stock never shows a shortfall: its `Verfügbar` stops at 0.
- **Stock movements.** Only managers record manual additions and removals at a location. No correction flow exists: a new addition or removal fixes a wrong booking. Stock cannot go below zero. Every movement records who, when, where, why, and the quantity before and after. A movement from job material also keeps its job or project. Users have no transfer flow.
- **Locations and categories.** Managers create their own locations, labeled Lager, room, shelf, vehicle or other. WerkFlow creates no default warehouse, so the inventory mirrors the real rooms, shelves and vehicles. Each organization starts with editable SHK categories. Category names carry no product logic.
- **Units.** An article's unit is its base unit: stock, job material and prices count in it. The unit comes from one fixed list, which also holds the units an offer needs without an article, such as Stunde, Pauschal, Kilometer and Tag. The article form and the template editor's „Artikel erstellen“ offer only units from the list, and a save with text that names no unit is refused instead of read as Stück. An article saved earlier with another unit keeps its stock and bookings, shows „Einheit prüfen“ in the catalog, and its form marks the unit until one from the list is chosen. Every unit accepts a decimal quantity.
- **CSV import.** The initial inventory at onboarding goes through CSV import with column mapping. A file with more data rows or columns than one import takes is refused when it is chosen, and the file field names the limit. The browser reads the file; only its rows and a hash of its content reach the server. When a file with the same content was booked before, the dialog names the date and the person who booked it and waits until the user continues or picks another file.
- **Import preview.** After the mapping, the preview lists every row with its effect, and nothing is booked before „Jetzt buchen“. The preview and the booking run the same database rule, so they agree on create or match. Matching checks the internal SKU first, then a barcode, also against articles an earlier row of the same file creates. A row shows whether it creates or matches an article, the unit as understood, the quantity per Lager, a Lager the import creates, and the resulting stock. Totals above the rows count new, matched and rejected rows and the stock each Lager receives per unit. A matched row adds its quantity to the article's stock as an `Eingang` and leaves the article as it is: no field, barcode, category, supplier or price changes. A new article gets the row's supplier as its first reference and the row's prices as CSV-import prices from the day of the booking. A new article's quantity is its first count, and the import creates a Lager only for a quantity it books.
- **Import refusals.** A row without a name, with an empty unit or with a unit outside the unit list is rejected and never becomes Stück. A matched row whose unit differs from the article's unit is rejected („Einheit weicht ab: Stück statt Meter“); the import converts no unit. A row whose barcode belongs to another article is rejected. A rejected row leaves nothing behind, not even its category, supplier or Lager. A row that fails for a reason the import does not name reads „Die Zeile konnte nicht übernommen werden“, the other rows still book, and the server logs the database error code with the number of such rows. A row with a quantity but no Lager keeps its article, books no quantity, and counts separately.
- **Stammdaten only.** „Nur Stammdaten und Preise“ creates articles with their prices and books no stock and creates no Lager, whatever the quantity column holds. The preview says so.
- **Import booking.** The booking books the previewed rows of its batch once: a confirm sent twice books once and answers the first result. It holds to what the preview showed: a row whose article match or Lager changed since the preview is rejected („Artikel oder Lager haben sich seit der Vorschau geändert“) instead of booking to another article or Lager, and a row the preview rejected stays rejected. Going back from the preview to the mapping checks the file for a repeat again. The result lists every rejected row and every row without a Lager with its reason, and offers them as a CSV report built in the browser, with the original cells and the file's line numbers. A booking with no such row closes the dialog with the counts.
- **Job and project material.** Managers plan catalog items on jobs and projects without changing stock. Planning is one database write that another write, such as a future offer conversion, can include; it plans an item once per named source. Managers and assigned employees take items from a location or return them. A take or return changes stock immediately, with no approval step. An employee may take an existing item that was never planned, and the line is marked unplanned. A refused take creates no unplanned line. A take on a line whose item a manager changed after it was opened is refused with "Die Materialposition wurde inzwischen geändert" and books nothing. A take, use or return whose answer was lost books once when sent again from the same dialog opening, also from a field sync replay under the same operation id: the repeat answers with the first booking. Sent again with another quantity, Lager or note, it is refused with „Diese Buchung wurde schon mit anderen Angaben gespeichert“ and books nothing. Projects show direct material, material from child jobs and the total separately. A job's material lines move with the job to another project or out of every project; a movement or recorded use keeps the project it was booked with. Lines keep planned, taken, used (`verbraucht`), returned and billable quantities apart. Nothing commercial uses the billable quantity. An employee reads neither the billable quantity nor the billing flag, not even through the Data API.
- **Used material.** Managers and assigned employees record what was used on a job line; a project's own lines stay with the office. Recording use moves no stock, because the material left the Lager at the take. Use and return together never exceed the take, also for two bookings at once. Each use is an append-only event with actor, time, quantity in the article's unit and the article's purchase price of that day per its price quantity; an article without a price leaves the cost empty. When the article's unit changed after a use was recorded, a new use on that line is refused until the office checks the line. A wrong use is corrected by a new negative event with a reason that names the event it corrects. No screen offers that correction yet. Employees never read the cost, not even through the Data API. A job or project whose material was taken or used cannot be deleted; the refusal says why and points to a status instead. A line with recorded use, even one corrected back to 0, can be neither removed nor cancelled. Admin and Büro read the consumption of a job or project, with every event and its cost, through one server reader for the coming billable review; it leaves out cancelled lines, which hold no use, and decides no billability.
- **Field material actions.** An assigned employee works with the job's material inside the field work pack. A take started on a planned line opens on that line alone; the catalog search opens only with „Weiteres Material“. A line offers „Verbraucht“ next to „Zurücklegen“ and states its facts in one line („5 m entnommen · 3 m verbraucht · 1 m zurück · 1 m noch offen“); „noch offen“ appears once something was used or returned. Before the booking, the use dialog names article, quantity, unit and that the stock stays the same. The unplanned-item search hides supplier, price, valuation and billability, and its hint names no supplier. Employees cannot create items, open `/inventar` or use project-level material.
- **Read-only consumers.** Templates, dispatch readiness, the work lifecycle, handover, service cases and maintenance plans show material facts and never create, reserve, consume or return stock. Readiness labels planned material as not reserved and tools as not assessed.
- **Tools and assets.** Tools and assets are catalog items. No screen creates, tracks or assigns an individual instance.

### Current limits

- The database does not yet refuse a unit outside the list; only the article form, its save action and the CSV import do.
- Not built: reservation, picking, approval, transfer, purchase order, goods receipt, supplier return, reorder worklist, formal stock count, valuation report, wholesale-standard integration, Excel import.
- The CSV import converts no unit, merges no duplicate articles and changes no data of a matched article, its prices included.
- A purchase unit comes from the article unit list, which has no „Stange“ or „Ring“ yet.
- No supplier comparison, purchase price per supplier, rebate group or supplier contact person exists.
- No completion gate checks material that is still open.
- No tool checkout, custody, maintenance, inspection, loss or retirement workflow exists.
