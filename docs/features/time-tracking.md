# Time tracking

Time tracking (`Zeiterfassung`) records attendance, work, travel, breaks, job allocation, standby and call-out, and turns them into time accounts, approved corrections, closed periods and a payroll-ready export. A field employee records the right kind of time with almost no effort, and the office, owner and payroll get one auditable result. It shows its calculation rules and gives no legal advice.

## Current Product Baseline

Every role clocks in and out, records work, travel, break, standby, call-out and internal-activity segments, and proposes corrections that a second person approves. Admin, Büro and effective holders of the time-approval responsibility review time and close monthly periods. Admin and Büro produce the payroll-ready export. Employees see their own history, balance and monthly statements.

### Capture

- **Clock readiness.** Clock actions stay disabled until the organization's clock state has loaded. A missing or failed response never means the employee is clocked out. A failure offers a retry, and a pending transition blocks duplicate actions. The [shared readiness contract](../technical/realtime-and-caching.md#shared-layout-and-clock-readiness) owns the mechanism.
- **The clock button.** A fixed button on every page opens a sheet of next actions, not a form. Each transition is one tap and carries the job along. Rare activities sit one level deeper. While clocked in, two hot keys offer the two most likely next transitions. After a break, resuming the interrupted job is one tap. The job picker lists today's planned jobs first and keeps its order while open, so no option moves under the finger.
- **What the clock button lacks, on purpose.** No clock-out hot key: a one-tap clock-out on a fixed button is an easy accidental tap, and an ended session has no undo. No global keyboard shortcut for the sheet (owner decision). The elapsed time shows only on the Zeiterfassung page, not on the button.
- **Live capture.** Each membership has one attendance session with at most one open activity. Every switch is atomic, safe to repeat and attributed. Nobody is clocked in in two organizations at once. Work, travel and call-out link to an assigned job or stay explicitly unallocated. Travel also records the route and whether the employee drove or rode along. Current-day totals and the split at midnight (Europe/Berlin) are display only.
- **Recovery.** A session open for more than a day shows a concrete recovery path. Sign-out closes the session and records who closed it. Time recorded before the segment model stays unchanged and is never backfilled.
- **Member removal.** Removing a member who has recorded time is refused, and membership, sessions and history stay unchanged. [Employee management](employee-management.md#current-product-baseline) owns the removal rule.
- **Break rule.** Admin chooses manually stamped breaks or one automatic break threshold and duration. Büro sees the rule. Saving a new rule also ends the open break of every member on a break today and resumes the job that break interrupted, in the same save. A closed period or a concurrent rule change refuses the whole save. A rule change never rewrites closed days.
- **Targets.** The dashboard shows presence, work, break and overtime per day against the daily and weekly target. Today's totals count every session of the day and the day's manual entries, before and after clock-out. The overview names the running activity, such as „Du bist gerade unterwegs.“, and lists travel, standby, call-out and internal time as parts of the working time beside the work, so the parts add up to it. On a day past its target the parts cover the working time with its overtime, shown as „Gesamt …, davon …“. [Employee management](employee-management.md#current-product-baseline) owns how schedules, holidays, closures, vacation and sickness set the target. Approved full-day vacation blocks clock-in. A sickness day only warns.
- **Manual entries.** Everyone can add same-day work or break entries, with sequence and overlap checks. Employee entries and a Büro user's own entries wait for approval. An Admin's own additions apply directly, as the owner's recovery path. A work entry may name a job of the organization; an employee's entry names only a job the employee is assigned to, as on the live clock. The database checks the assignment again when it saves the entry.

### Approval and corrections

- **Approval authority.** Effective holders of the time-approval responsibility decide pending time. Without configuration, Admin decides Büro and employee time, and Büro decides employee time. Named holders replace that default, and a substitute inherits the holder's scope for a date window. Self-approval never exists. Every review surface, the calendar's entry details included, offers a decision only where this authority allows it. Pending entries group per manual submission for one day. A single decision and approve-all both re-check each entry's pending state and the approver's authority. A review or deletion of several entries applies to all or to none.
- **Corrections.** A guided `Zeitkorrektur` covers add, edit, delete, split, activity reclassification, job reallocation, employee reassignment and missed clocks. An employee's own change is always a proposal. An authorized Admin or Büro user may apply a correction for another person directly. Their own correction still needs a second approver. A correction offers every open job of the organization, because the office reviews each one.
- **Corrections over midnight.** Time that runs past midnight (Europe/Berlin), such as a forgotten night call-out, goes in as one correction. Its proposal closes and reopens at 00:00, as live capture shows it. Each day is checked for overlaps and closed periods on its own. Time over midnight may stay open for at most 24 hours, the length live capture flags as unusually long; a longer proposal is refused with that rule. A correction that moves only the start or only the end of a time across midnight is refused with that rule, never as an overlap.
- **Own entries (owner decision).** Büro changes their own recorded time only through a `Zeitkorrektur` with a second approver. The direct edit and deletion refuse an own entry, in the database too. An Admin may edit or delete their own entries directly. Each such change records who, when, and the entry before and after, in the same transaction. Admin and Büro see these records under `Verlauf`, below the time entries of the chosen range.
- **Correction lifecycle.** A request is in review, returned with a question, resubmitted, approved, rejected, withdrawn or failed to apply. Nothing is erased. Selected approvals apply all or nothing. The approval queue reads only submitted requests. The history pages on the server, newest first, and counts only the requests the viewer may see. Pending proposals are labeled provisional everywhere; the overview's „Vorgemerkte Zeit“ names the days they change. Confirmed views show only accepted results. The original capture history stays intact.
- **Attention.** Time and vacation approvals count toward the viewer's Zeiterfassung badge and appear in `Aufgaben` for exactly the effective approvers. Decisions run only through the review actions.

### Accounts, periods and export

- **Time accounts.** Each organization has one versioned default credited-time policy, plus optional employee-specific policies. Credit rules weight each activity category and never change recorded minutes. Standby, call-out, travel and the other activities get 0, 50 or 100 percent credit. Night, Sunday and holiday values are classifications only. Every in-scope person, people without a login included, has an explicitly opened account with opening balance, date and reason. The rule and account setup proposes the first day of the oldest month without a closed period as start date and says why: a month counts a rule and an account only from their start. An Admin corrects a wrong opening date with a reason while no closed period ends on or after the old or the new date. The opening balance moves with the date, and the history keeps every earlier date with its reason. Employees see their own account and statements under `Zeitkonto`. Adjustment, expiry and payout are manual events with four-eyes approval.
- **Period close.** A period is one organization-wide calendar month in Europe/Berlin and covers the whole workforce. A missing balance, schedule, policy or absence classification blocks close for that person instead of counting as zero. A period cannot close while a session that started before its end still runs. The refusal names the employees. It cannot close while a correction request that proposes a time in the period is open. The close checks this itself and does not rely on the preparation's findings. Close writes an immutable version. Recalculation and re-close create a successor version.
- **Closed periods.** Any write of recorded time whose day or new day lies in a closed period fails until an Admin reopens the period with a reason: a manual entry, an entry review, an edit, a deletion or a correction. The database refuses the write too, so no path around the actions changes a closed month.
- **Payroll export.** Admin and Büro generate and download one reproducible payroll-ready ZIP per closed period version, from a confirmed employee and code mapping. The export covers the whole workforce of that version and records that scope. A re-export explicitly supersedes the earlier one. A failed export ends as failed, and the period can be exported again. An export that stopped part way and stayed unchanged counts as failed at the next export of its period. A time-approval holder who is neither Admin nor Büro manages the period but sees no export and cannot fetch one.
- **Findings and adjustments.** Effective time approvers resolve findings, approve overtime candidates and account adjustments, and close periods in `Perioden`. A finding or an adjustment of a person follows the approval authority above. A person without a login counts as an employee. Whoever proposed an adjustment never decides it, but may withdraw it while it is open. The history records a withdrawal as its own step, not as a decision. Admin and Büro see the open requests beside the proposal forms. The database enforces these rules too. A finding decision reaches other open sessions of the period page live.

### Shared rules

- **Failed reads.** A failed read on the Zeiterfassung and Perioden pages shows the failure with a retry, never an empty list or a hidden tab.
- **Access suspension.** Suspending a member removes all their access and deletes no time record, session, statement or closed period.
- **Organization deletion.** Recorded time or a closed period blocks deleting the organization ([employee management](employee-management.md#current-product-baseline)).
- **Connected views.** Calendar time blocks, job and project time views and the field work pack show the same accepted time. The work pack uses the global session and has no job-local timer. Moving a plan never creates or rewrites actual time. An office handover may include a customer-safe time summary. An active clock blocks the handover, and handover review never edits or approves time.

### Current limits

- Change requests from before the guided correction flow keep their old meaning and are not converted.
- No automatic cap, expiry, payout, forfeiture or compensatory-time request exists.
- Arbitrary percentages, organization-defined categories, premiums and wage values are out of scope.
- Warnings for breaks, daily duration, rest and night, Sunday or holiday work are review aids, not proof of compliance with German law, a tariff or an employment agreement.
- The payroll handoff is a generic ZIP. It is not a provider integration, payroll calculation, accounting export or PDF payslip. No per-employee filter or differential export exists.
- Vacation and sickness are the only absence types, and absence is full-day only.
- No native mobile app or offline time queue exists. Do not describe web behavior as offline-capable.
