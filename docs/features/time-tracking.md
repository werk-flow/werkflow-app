# Time tracking

Time tracking (`Zeiterfassung`) records attendance, work, travel, breaks, job allocation, standby and call-out, and turns them into time accounts, approved corrections, closed periods and a payroll-ready export. A field employee records the right kind of time with almost no effort, and the office, owner and payroll get one auditable result. It shows its calculation rules and gives no legal advice.

## Current Product Baseline

Every role clocks in and out, records work, travel, break, standby, call-out and internal-activity segments, and proposes corrections that a second person approves. Admin, Büro and effective holders of the time-approval responsibility review time and close monthly periods. Admin and Büro produce the payroll-ready export. Employees see their own history, balance and monthly statements.

### Capture

- **Clock readiness.** Clock actions stay disabled until the organization's clock state has loaded. A missing or failed response never means the employee is clocked out. A failure offers a retry, and a pending transition blocks duplicate actions. The [shared readiness contract](../technical/realtime-and-caching.md#shared-layout-and-clock-readiness) owns the mechanism.
- **The clock dock.** A bar at the bottom of every page holds the clock button and names the running activity and its job by title, number and customer, so same-titled jobs stay apart, or says „Nicht eingestempelt“. Every page ends above the dock, so the dock never covers a control at any scroll position. The button opens a sheet of next actions, not a form. Each transition is one tap and carries the job along. Rare activities sit one level deeper. While clocked in, two hot keys in the dock offer the two most likely next transitions. After a break, resuming the interrupted job is one tap. The job picker lists the caller's job in progress first, then their visits today, their next visits by date, their other jobs and, for Admin and Büro, everyone else's, each group under its heading. The order holds across pages and while the picker is open, so no option moves under the finger. It never offers a parked job, and the database refuses a clock-in, switch or resume onto one with „Dieser Auftrag ist geparkt oder abgeschlossen.“
- **What the clock dock lacks, on purpose.** No clock-out hot key: a one-tap clock-out on a fixed control is an easy accidental tap, and an ended session has no undo. No global keyboard shortcut for the sheet (owner decision). The elapsed time shows only on the Zeiterfassung page, not in the dock.
- **Live capture.** Each membership has one attendance session with at most one open activity. Every switch is atomic, safe to repeat and attributed. Nobody is clocked in in two organizations at once. Work, travel and call-out link to an assigned job or stay explicitly unallocated. Travel also records the route and whether the employee drove or rode along. Current-day totals and the split at midnight (Europe/Berlin) are display only.
- **Offline-captured time.** The server accepts a clock start, switch or end that a phone captured without signal through the field sync route ([decision record 0010](../decisions/0010-offline-field-sync-contract.md)). It records the event at the phone's captured time, or at its arrival when the phone's clock ran ahead, and marks it offline-captured with the captured, sent and received times. Each operation is recorded once; a resend gets the first answer. Within 24 hours of its captured time the event is accepted automatically. Between 24 hours and 7 days the server records nothing and answers that the event needs a `Zeitkorrektur`, which the person files through the usual dialog. After 7 days, the recording deadline of the minimum wage law, the server refuses it. It also refuses a captured time in a closed period, more than 5 minutes in the future, or before the person's last recorded time, and a start onto a job the office blocked meanwhile. Every refusal reaches the phone as a stable code, and nothing is dropped. Each replay checks membership, role, the session's owner, the job assignment and closed periods again when it arrives. No phone sends these events yet.
- **Recovery.** A session open for more than a day shows a concrete recovery path. Sign-out closes the session and records who closed it. Time recorded before the segment model stays unchanged and is never backfilled.
- **Member removal.** Removing a member who has recorded time is refused, and membership, sessions and history stay unchanged. [Employee management](employee-management.md#current-product-baseline) owns the removal rule.
- **Break rule.** Admin chooses manually stamped breaks or one automatic break threshold and duration. Büro sees the rule. Saving a new rule also ends the open break of every member on a break today and resumes the job that break interrupted, in the same save. A closed period or a concurrent rule change refuses the whole save. A rule change never rewrites closed days.
- **Targets.** The dashboard shows presence, work, break and overtime per day against the daily and weekly target. Today's totals count every session of the day and the day's manual entries, before and after clock-out. The overview names the running activity, such as „Du bist gerade unterwegs.“, and lists travel, standby, call-out and internal time as parts of the working time beside the work, so the parts add up to it. On a day past its target the parts cover the working time with its overtime, shown as „Gesamt …, davon …“. [Employee management](employee-management.md#current-product-baseline) owns how schedules, holidays, closures, vacation and sickness set the target. Approved full-day vacation blocks clock-in. A sickness day only warns.
- **Manual entries.** An employee adds forgotten time through one action, „Zeit nachtragen“, in the Zeiterfassung header and the calendar. Its times start empty, so an untouched form sends nothing. Time of today becomes a manual entry; time of an earlier day needs a reason and becomes a `Zeitkorrektur` with the day's timeline (below), and the server refuses an employee's manual entry for an earlier day. Admin and Büro keep „Manuelle Eintragung“, which also enters other people's time and earlier days. Every manual entry passes sequence and overlap checks. Employee entries and a Büro user's own entries wait for approval. Until the decision, the author withdraws the whole submission with „Zurückziehen“ in `Verlauf`; its entries leave the time record, and a withdrawal record keeps them. An Admin's own additions apply directly, as the owner's recovery path. A work entry may name a job of the organization; an employee's entry names only a job the employee is assigned to, as on the live clock. The database checks the assignment again when it saves the entry. An entry that would leave a person clocked in while they are clocked in at another organization is refused. The refusal names that organization only to a caller who is a member of it; everyone else reads „in einer anderen Organisation“.

### Approval and corrections

- **Approval authority.** Effective holders of the time-approval responsibility decide pending time. Without configuration, Admin decides Büro and employee time, and Büro decides employee time. Named holders replace that default, and a substitute inherits the holder's scope for a date window. Self-approval never exists. Every review surface, the calendar's entry details included, offers a decision only where this authority allows it. Pending entries group per manual submission for one day. A single decision and approve-all both re-check the approver's authority over each person, then each entry's pending state, so only an approver learns that an entry was already decided. A review decides only entries of the active organization and refuses any other entry as not found. A review or deletion of several entries applies to all or to none.
- **Corrections.** A guided `Zeitkorrektur` covers added time, edit, delete, split, activity reclassification, job reallocation and employee reassignment. Added time is one kind; only Admin and Büro choose whose time it is. An employee's own change is always a proposal. An authorized Admin or Büro user may apply a correction for another person directly. Their own correction still needs a second approver. A correction offers every open job of the organization, because the office reviews each one, by title, and marks the caller's own jobs „Dir zugewiesen“. An employee finds these jobs by number and title only and never sees a job's description; a finished job appears only where the employee's own time names it. Each proposed time belongs to the person the correction is for. Only a reassignment names another person, and only Admin and Büro reassign time (owner decision); the server refuses any other such proposal before it reads recorded time. A retry after a lost answer saves nothing twice. When the correction changed since the lost send, the dialog and „Zeit nachtragen“ say that the earlier version stands and the changes are not saved, instead of reporting a save.
- **Added time as a timeline.** Added time, the office's „Zeit nachtragen“ in `Verlauf` and an employee's earlier day alike, is one proposal of the day's activities in order: work, travel, break, standby, call-out and internal activity, each with its times, its job and the details live capture asks for. The person's planned job visits of that day prefill it until the person changes it; only Admin and Büro read another person's visits. „Pause einfügen“ splits an activity around a 30-minute break. A break lies directly between two activities, and a gap between two activities clocks out. An end before its start is refused unless the activity is marked „Endet am nächsten Tag“, so a typed-over time never becomes a long night. The form warns, and never inserts a break, when more than 6 hours of work carry less than 30 minutes of break, or more than 9 hours less than 45 minutes, counting breaks and gaps of at least 15 minutes (ArbZG § 4); every activity but a break counts as work, as in the period warning. The server refuses added travel, standby or internal time without its details. Each correction card lists the recorded and the proposed activities with their times and job.
- **Corrections over midnight.** Time that runs past midnight (Europe/Berlin), such as a forgotten night call-out, goes in as one correction. Its proposal closes and reopens at 00:00, as live capture shows it. Each day is checked for overlaps and closed periods on its own. Time over midnight may stay open for at most 24 hours, the length live capture flags as unusually long; a longer proposal is refused with that rule. A correction that moves only the start or only the end of a time across midnight is refused with that rule, never as an overlap.
- **Own entries (owner decision).** Büro changes their own recorded time only through a `Zeitkorrektur` with a second approver. The direct edit and deletion refuse an own entry, in the database too. An Admin may edit or delete their own entries directly. Each such change records who, when, and the entry before and after, in the same transaction. Admin and Büro see these records under `Verlauf`, below the time entries of the chosen range.
- **Correction lifecycle.** A request is in review, returned with a question, resubmitted, approved, rejected, withdrawn or failed to apply. Nothing is erased. Selected approvals apply all or nothing. The approval queue reads only submitted requests. The history pages on the server, newest first, and counts only the requests the viewer may see. Pending proposals are labeled provisional everywhere; the overview's „Vorgemerkte Zeit“ sums the pending corrections and manual submissions and names the days they change, and a legacy time longer than a week by its first and last day. Confirmed views show only accepted results. The original capture history stays intact.
- **Attention.** Time and vacation approvals count toward the viewer's Zeiterfassung badge and appear in `Aufgaben` for exactly the effective approvers. Decisions run only through the review actions.

### Accounts, periods and export

- **Time accounts.** Each organization has one versioned default credited-time policy, plus optional employee-specific policies. A day follows the policy version with the latest start on or before it; the rules card lists every version with its span and marks the one in force today. Credit rules weight each activity category and never change recorded minutes. Standby, call-out, travel and the other activities get 0, 50 or 100 percent credit. Night, Sunday and holiday values are classifications only. Every in-scope person, people without a login included, has an explicitly opened account with opening balance, date and reason. An Admin opens one account or all missing ones together with one shared date and reason, each balance typed as „±HH:MM“. The opening applies to everyone or no one, records each person separately, and refuses a date on or before the end of a closed period or more than ten years back. The card says that a period covers everyone employed in its month and shows each person's entry and exit date. The rule and account setup proposes the first day of the oldest month without a closed period as start date and says why: a month counts a rule and an account only from their start. An Admin corrects a wrong opening date with a reason while no closed period ends on or after the old or the new date. The opening balance moves with the date, and the history keeps every earlier date with its reason. Employees see their own account and statements under `Zeitkonto`. Adjustment, expiry and payout are manual events with four-eyes approval.
- **Period close.** A period is one organization-wide calendar month in Europe/Berlin and covers the whole workforce. A missing balance, schedule, policy or absence classification blocks close for that person instead of counting as zero. While a person's policy or account is missing, `Monatswerte` shows „—“ with „Nicht berechnet“ and the cause instead of values. „Arbeitsplan fehlt“ comes from the person's Wochenplan alone, never from a missing policy or account. `Monatswerte` lists people by name, in the same order at every preparation. „Monat abschließen“ stays enabled; a press while something blocks the close names the blockers instead of closing. A period cannot close while a session that started before its end still runs. The refusal names the employees. It cannot close while a correction request that proposes a time in the period is open. The close checks this itself and does not rely on the preparation's findings. A finding that needs approval counts only when its latest decision, the one `Perioden` shows, approves it. An approval is final; a rejection stays open to a later approval. A rejection that older data holds after an approval still blocks the close; preparing the period again creates a new finding to approve. Close writes an immutable version. Recalculation and re-close create a successor version. An employee with a result in a version reads that version without the organization's balance totals, also through the Data API.
- **Closed periods.** Any write of recorded time whose day or new day lies in a closed period fails until an Admin reopens the period with a reason: a manual entry, an entry review, an edit, a deletion or a correction. The database refuses the write too, so no path around the actions changes a closed month.
- **Payroll export.** Admin and Büro generate and download one reproducible payroll-ready ZIP per closed period version, from a confirmed employee and code mapping. An Admin sees and edits the Lohnart code of every classification before confirming; each save is a new mapping version, and an export keeps the version it was built from. A save from a table that shows an older version is refused. The export covers the whole workforce of that version and records that scope. A re-export explicitly supersedes the earlier one. A failed export ends as failed, and the period can be exported again. An export that stopped part way and stayed unchanged counts as failed at the next export of its period. A time-approval holder who is neither Admin nor Büro manages the period but sees no export and cannot fetch one.
- **Findings and adjustments.** Effective time approvers resolve findings, approve overtime candidates and account adjustments, and close periods in `Perioden`. A finding or an adjustment of a person follows the approval authority above. A person without a login counts as an employee. Whoever proposed an adjustment never decides it, but may withdraw it while it is open. The history records a withdrawal as its own step, not as a decision. Admin and Büro see the open requests beside the proposal forms. The database enforces these rules too. A finding decision reaches other open sessions of the period page live. `Prüfhinweise` shows one card per kind with every affected person, what fixes it and a link to the screen that does: the Zeitkonto rules for a missing policy or account (Admin), the person's Arbeitszeitmodell for a missing Wochenplan (Admin and Büro), the history for a missing clock or an overlap, and the approvals for an open correction. A viewer without the right sees „Nur Admin“ or „Nur Admin und Büro“ instead of the link. „Zeit ohne Zuordnung“ lists each entry with its day, time, activity and minutes, and its reason field has a visible label.

### Shared rules

- **Failed reads.** A failed read on the Zeiterfassung and Perioden pages shows the failure with a retry, never an empty list or a hidden tab.
- **Access suspension.** Suspending a member removes all their access and deletes no time record, session, statement or closed period.
- **Organization deletion.** Recorded time or a closed period blocks deleting the organization ([employee management](employee-management.md#current-product-baseline)).
- **Activities.** A job's time, the field work pack and an employee's `Verlauf` read recorded time as activities: each start ends at the next clock-out or break of that day, whatever job that event names, so a legacy clock-out without a job still ends the job's time. Only today's open activity „läuft“; an earlier start without an end, or a start that another start follows, reads „Ende fehlt“, as the totals leave it uncounted, and an end without a start „Beginn fehlt“. An activity shorter than a minute never shows as a row, a calendar block or a block's segment; the Zeiterfassung totals and the time account still count it.
- **An employee's `Verlauf`.** An employee reads their own days, newest first, each with one row per activity in time order: kind, from–to, duration and job, with „Korrigieren“ or „Zurückziehen“ on the row, and „Ende korrigieren“ where the end is an entry of its own. The status filter keeps whole activities: an activity with a pending end is pending. Admin and Büro keep one row per entry with person, type, timestamp, status and review date.
- **Connected views.** Calendar time blocks, job and project time views and the field work pack show the same accepted time. The work pack uses the global session and has no job-local timer. Moving a plan never creates or rewrites actual time. An office handover may include a customer-safe time summary. An active clock blocks the handover, and handover review never edits or approves time.

### Current limits

- Change requests from before the guided correction flow keep their old meaning and are not converted.
- No automatic cap, expiry, payout, forfeiture or compensatory-time request exists.
- Arbitrary percentages, organization-defined categories, premiums and wage values are out of scope.
- Warnings for breaks, daily duration, rest and night, Sunday or holiday work are review aids, not proof of compliance with German law, a tariff or an employment agreement.
- The payroll handoff is a generic ZIP. It is not a provider integration, payroll calculation, accounting export or PDF payslip. No per-employee filter or differential export exists.
- Vacation and sickness are the only absence types, and absence is full-day only.
- No native mobile app or offline time queue exists. Do not describe web behavior as offline-capable.
