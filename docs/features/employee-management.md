# Employee management

Employee management is the relationship between an organization and its people: membership, access, personnel data, employment conditions, schedules, qualifications, leave and personnel documents. It answers who works here, with which access, who is available and qualified for an `Auftrag`, and which conditions apply on a date. It replaces personnel spreadsheets and paper folders without becoming a generic HR suite.

## Current Product Baseline

Every person in an organization has one personnel record with employment conditions, a work schedule, responsibilities, vacation and sickness, teams and qualifications, a time account, protected personnel documents and an access and employment lifecycle. Admin and Büro manage people. Employees act on their own data. A future starter sees their own onboarding before the start date.

- **Roles.** Creating an organization makes the creator its Admin, with a personnel record and default settings, in one step. If any part fails, no organization remains. The fixed roles show as `Admin`, `Büro` and `Handwerker/in`. Nobody changes their own role, appoints a second admin or removes themselves. Büro manages employees but not peers or admins. Custom roles and per-field permissions are absent by decision. Creating a further organization needs an active subscription: the create dialog says so before the name field.
- **Settings.** Every role has its profile, account, own `Aufträge` columns and own responsibilities and substitutes under Einstellungen. Admin and Büro also see the organization sections, and Büro only reads what Admin changes. An employee sees no organization section; opening one sends them to their profile, so no read of theirs is refused.
- **Organization deletion.** Only the owner deletes the organization, after typing its name. The confirmation counts what goes: memberships, open invitations, customers, projects, jobs, personnel records and stored files. The database decides first: if it refuses, no file is touched and the organization stays. The stored files stay long enough for a database restore to find them ([storage cleanup](../technical/document-storage-and-access.md#storage-cleanup)). Its responsibility groups go with it, so the last holder of a named group does not block the deletion. Recorded working time, a closed period or a document inside its retention period refuses the deletion, in the database too: the dialog names each reason with its retention duty, and for recorded time points to `Perioden` for the payroll export (owner decision).
- **Organization export.** Only an Admin opens „Datenexport“ under Einstellungen and exports the whole organization, for an exit, an audit or the company's own archive. The export holds every record as a CSV file for German Excel with German column names, a document directory that lists each document once with its folder path, links, category, document date, retention, filed state, origin and the place of every version in the archive, and every original file with its earlier versions, protected personnel files included. The directory notes a document deleted or given a new version between the start and the data part's build; a document uploaded after the start appears among the records only if it came before the data part was built, never with its original file, and a new export captures later changes. The data part reads the record areas one after another, so changes made while it builds can leave related records from slightly different moments; for an exact copy, export while nobody works in WerkFlow. Join and invite codes stay out. A large organization gets several parts, each with every file in exactly one part. The page lists the five latest exports and every older one with a part still to build or download. The screen shows each part as pending, being built, ready or failed; a failed part says why and builds again alone, unless a new build cannot help, such as for a missing original. A part not built within 7 days after the export started can no longer be built; a new export then takes its place. Each part stays downloadable for 7 days after its build began and is then [due for deletion](../technical/document-storage-and-access.md#storage-cleanup); deleting the organization makes it due at once or, while a build may still store it, when that build's write window ends. Every start, part result and download is recorded in a history nobody can change. Büro and employees neither see nor open the section, and a part of another organization answers like an unknown one.
- **Invitations and member list.** Admin and Büro invite Büro or employee members by email, manage pending invitations and see each member's clock status and daily progress. Employees cannot open the member list. An invite link makes the person a member at once. Inviting a person without a login replaces their pending invite and connects the new one to their personnel record in one save, before any mail goes out. When the invite mail fails, no invite stays behind, and the personnel record keeps its previous invite. A failed read of the list or of a personnel section shows the failure with a retry.
- **Join requests.** Entering the organization code creates a join request, not a membership. The person waits outside the app. Each person has at most one open request and can withdraw it. Admin and Büro see open requests in `Aufgaben` and decide them in the Mitarbeiter area. Approval adds the person as `Handwerker/in`, and the waiting person enters the app without reloading. After a decline, the person sees it and can enter another code. Every code entry counts against an hourly limit. Invite links stay immediate.
- **Personnel record.** Admin and Büro maintain employee number, contact and emergency data, entry and exit dates and notes, and see every change in a history. People without a login are personnel records too. Inviting such a person later connects the account to the existing record without a duplicate. For people with a login, the profile name is authoritative.
- **Employment conditions.** Employment type, weekly hours and yearly vacation days are date-effective versions. The newest version on or before a date applies, so past work keeps its meaning. WerkFlow stores no compensation, by decision. A record without a controlled lifecycle shows employment and access labels derived from its dates and login state.
- **Work schedules and holidays.** Each person has date-effective weekly patterns with minutes per weekday. For time targets, the schedule wins over the weekly hours. Admin selects the holiday region. Admin and Büro maintain company closure days. Only today and future days can change. The daily target comes from the schedule, else the weekly hours spread over Monday to Friday, else a visibly labeled 8-hour default on Monday to Friday. Days before the entry date or after the exit date have no target. Holidays and closure days set it to 0. Every surface, the Plantafel, the Zeitkonto and the Perioden included, reads this one target. One exception remains: vacation counting does not yet apply the entry and exit dates, so a weekday after the exit date can still cost entitlement. The member list and detail measure today's progress against it, and say so when it could not be read instead of assuming 8 hours.
- **Scoped responsibilities.** The organization owner decides who approves time and who approves vacation: the role default (Admin and Büro), or a named group of holders that replaces it. Naming a holder grants no other manager access. The server checks authority when the action runs. Nobody approves their own request. Every change shows its effect before it is saved.
- **Substitutes.** A holder names a substitute for an inclusive date window. The substitute gets exactly that holder's scope. It ends when the window ends or the holder's access is suspended or ended, even if a browser still shows the old view. The owner's substitute picker shows each person's role and how many days of the window they are absent, from full-day approved vacation and reported sickness, without the type. A substitute absent on every day of the window shows a warning, and the save reads „Trotzdem speichern“. When the absence was approved after the dialog opened, the save itself shows the warning, and a second save accepts it. A running window ends today; a window that has not started is removed and never takes effect, and its record stays. A window ends once: a second end, from another tab, changes nothing. Every section lists holders by name. Affected people see their responsibilities and substitutions in their settings.
- **Vacation.** Employees request and withdraw their own vacation, from January 1 of last year to December 31 of next year. Vacation approvers decide requests and cancel approved vacation with a reason. Entitlement comes from the employment condition. Only days with a positive target consume it. The balance is plain arithmetic, or a label that no entitlement exists. An employee's own balance splits approved days into taken and „verplant“: days after today are planned, not taken, and both lower the remaining entitlement. A running vacation's approved days count as taken through today first. Approved vacation lowers the daily target and blocks clock-in that day.
- **Sickness.** A sickness report is a fact, not a request. Employees report themselves. Admin and Büro can record one for someone. A correction changes the same report and needs a reason. There is no diagnosis field, by design. Only the person and Admin or Büro see sickness type and evidence status. The shared calendar shows a neutral absence. Active sickness sets the target to 0 and does not block clock-in.
- **Work an absence affects.** When Admin or Büro record a sickness, the dialog stays open on the person's scheduled visits in the period, two weeks ahead for an open end: date, job and the person's dispatch state, each linking to its day on the Plantafel. An approved vacation shows the same list to Admin and Büro until they close it. A failed read says the absence is saved and offers a retry. Until the planners act, each visit an approved vacation still takes its person away from is an „Einsatz umplanen“ task in `Aufgaben` for Admin and Büro: person, visit, day and dispatch state, linking to that day on the Plantafel, without the kind of absence. A visit and person give one task, however many vacations reach the visit. The task ends once the visit no longer has that person, or the vacation is withdrawn or cancelled. A past visit and a recorded sickness give no task.
- **Teams.** Teams are date-effective planning shortcuts and grant no rights. Picking a team in an assignment adds the members active on that date.
- **Qualifications.** Admin and Büro maintain a catalog of skills and certifications, assign entries with validity and evidence status and set requirements on jobs. Every assignment checks coverage on the planned date. A gap can be overridden only with a recorded reason. „Intern bestätigt“ is an operational fact, not a legal claim. Employees see their own entries read-only. Expiring certificates appear in `Aufgaben`.
- **Attention.** `Aufgaben` shows the approvals a person can decide now, decision notifications and the person's own requests. Time-bound decisions lead: time approvals, then vacation requests, then visits to replan, then open Anfragen. The handover backlog comes last, as a count with its oldest few rows and „Alle anzeigen“. Each approval and open Anfrage says how long it has waited. A badge never counts an item the viewer cannot act on.
- **Dispatch acknowledgement.** Employees confirm or challenge the current revision of a dispatch. An acknowledgement never stands in for attendance or recorded time.
- **Time facts.** Managers inspect time but never record live time for someone else. A correction of one's own time always needs a second time approver. [Time tracking](time-tracking.md) owns the time rules.
- **Time accounts.** Every in-scope personnel record, with or without a login, has an explicitly opened time account. A missing opening balance, schedule or policy blocks the period close for that person and never counts as zero. Employees see their own account and monthly statements.
- **Access and employment lifecycle.** Admin plans activation, suspends, reactivates or ends organization access, and runs employment transitions. None of these deletes the record, its history or the login. A record without a lifecycle is labeled as not controlled. The owner and the last effective Admin are protected. Suspension and access end stop every read at once, in the app and in the database. Before the start date a future starter reads only their own onboarding, not their `Zeitkonto`. The last active holder of a selected responsibility group blocks a transition that pauses or ends their employment until someone else takes it over. A holder who has an exit date, a planned future one included, or who left the organization counts as no holder.
- **Onboarding.** Organization templates have immutable published versions. A plan created from a template holds editable requirements that reference existing documents, qualifications, conditions, schedules, teams or acknowledgements. Only a requirement marked as blocking access delays activation. The person confirms only an acknowledgement requirement themselves; Admin or Büro completes every other type, in the app and in the database. Missing configuration never shows as complete.
- **Protected personnel documents.** Personnel files have three classes: standard, Admin-only and health evidence. They sit outside the ordinary library. No responsibility, job assignment or document permission grants access to them. Admin sees every class. Büro manages standard files. The person sees only expressly released versions, without changing them, and can upload requested health evidence. A Büro member's own Admin-only file stays read-only to them. An acknowledgement proves that the person saw one exact version. It is not a signature.
- **Removal and account deletion.** A member can be removed only before any time was recorded, so membership and time history survive. For a member with recorded time, the office ends employment and access through the personnel record. A removal marks the personnel record as exited today in the same step, so a refused removal changes nothing. A member with a future entry date is removed only after that date is corrected. A person deletes their own account only while they hold no membership, suspended or ended included. The database refuses deleting the account of a member with recorded time, and a refused deletion keeps the profile.

### Current limits

- [Calendar and resource planning](calendar-and-resource-planning.md#current-product-baseline) owns capacity conflicts. Employee management shows only absence signals. Minimum staffing, shift rotations and date-specific schedule overrides do not exist.
- Vacation and sickness are the only absence types.
- Time-account adjustments, expiry and payout are manual four-eyes events. WerkFlow applies no automatic cap, expiry, payout or money calculation. Time-account balances carry forward. Vacation carryover does not exist.
- Attention is in-app only. No reminder, escalation, notification preference or external delivery exists.
- A sole admin's own vacation request has no eligible approver until named vacation approvers exist.
- Employees cannot propose corrections to their own master data or conditions. Self-service covers vacation, sickness, acknowledgements, released documents and requested evidence.
- Ownership transfer and emergency owner recovery do not exist.
- A member who joins a further organization of the same owner sees it after the next reload or tab return, not live. A join request records who decided and when, but no reason.
- No compensation, payroll profile, payslip, payroll-provider integration, electronic signature or legal retention exists. The only payroll output is a generic payroll-ready export with employee and code mapping.
- The organization export builds only while the Admin's page is open, and nothing imports an export back.
