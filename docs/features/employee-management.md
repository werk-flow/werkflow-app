# Employee management

Employee management is the relationship between an organization and its people: membership, access, personnel data, employment conditions, schedules, qualifications, leave and personnel documents. It answers who works here, with which access, who is available and qualified for an `Auftrag`, and which conditions apply on a date. It replaces personnel spreadsheets and paper folders without becoming a generic HR suite.

## Current Product Baseline

Every person in an organization has one personnel record with employment conditions, a work schedule, responsibilities, vacation and sickness, teams and qualifications, a time account, protected personnel documents and an access and employment lifecycle. Admin and Büro manage people. Employees act on their own data. A future starter sees their own onboarding before the start date.

- **Roles.** Creating an organization makes the creator its Admin, with a personnel record and default settings, in one step. If any part fails, no organization remains. The fixed roles show as `Admin`, `Büro` and `Handwerker/in`. Nobody changes their own role, appoints a second admin or removes themselves. Büro manages employees but not peers or admins. Custom roles and per-field permissions are absent by decision. Creating a further organization needs an active subscription: the create dialog says so before the name field.
- **Settings.** Every role has its profile, account, own `Aufträge` columns and own responsibilities and substitutes under Einstellungen. Admin and Büro also see the organization sections, and Büro only reads what Admin changes. An employee sees no organization section; opening one sends them to their profile, so no read of theirs is refused.
- **Organization deletion.** Only the owner deletes the organization, after typing its name. The confirmation counts what goes: memberships, open invitations, customers, projects, jobs, personnel records and stored files. The files go first; if that fails, the organization stays and the owner can try again. Recorded working time or a closed period refuses the deletion, in the database too: the dialog names the retention duty and points to `Perioden` for the payroll export (owner decision).
- **Invitations and member list.** Admin and Büro invite Büro or employee members by email, manage pending invitations and see each member's clock status and daily progress. Employees cannot open the member list. An invite link makes the person a member at once. Inviting a person without a login replaces their pending invite and connects the new one to their personnel record in one save, before any mail goes out. When the invite mail fails, no invite stays behind, and the personnel record keeps its previous invite. A failed read of the list or of a personnel section shows the failure with a retry.
- **Join requests.** Entering the organization code creates a join request, not a membership. The person waits outside the app. Each person has at most one open request and can withdraw it. Admin and Büro see open requests in `Aufgaben` and decide them in the Mitarbeiter area. Approval adds the person as `Handwerker/in`, and the waiting person enters the app without reloading. After a decline, the person sees it and can enter another code. Wrong codes count against an hourly limit. Invite links stay immediate.
- **Personnel record.** Admin and Büro maintain employee number, contact and emergency data, entry and exit dates and notes, and see every change in a history. People without a login are personnel records too. Inviting such a person later connects the account to the existing record without a duplicate. For people with a login, the profile name is authoritative.
- **Employment conditions.** Employment type, weekly hours and yearly vacation days are date-effective versions. The newest version on or before a date applies, so past work keeps its meaning. WerkFlow stores no compensation, by decision. A record without a controlled lifecycle shows employment and access labels derived from its dates and login state.
- **Work schedules and holidays.** Each person has date-effective weekly patterns with minutes per weekday. For time targets, the schedule wins over the weekly hours. Admin selects the holiday region. Admin and Büro maintain company closure days. Only today and future days can change. The daily target comes from the schedule, else the weekly hours, else a visibly labeled 8-hour default. Holidays and closure days set it to 0.
- **Scoped responsibilities.** The organization owner decides who approves time and who approves vacation: the role default (Admin and Büro), or a named group of holders that replaces it. Naming a holder grants no other manager access. The server checks authority when the action runs. Nobody approves their own request. Every change shows its effect before it is saved.
- **Substitutes.** A holder names a substitute for an inclusive date window. The substitute gets exactly that holder's scope. It ends when the window ends or the holder's access is suspended or ended, even if a browser still shows the old view. Affected people see their responsibilities and substitutions in their settings.
- **Vacation.** Employees request and withdraw their own vacation, from January 1 of last year to December 31 of next year. Vacation approvers decide requests and cancel approved vacation with a reason. Entitlement comes from the employment condition. Only days with a positive target consume it. The balance is plain arithmetic, or a label that no entitlement exists. Approved vacation lowers the daily target and blocks clock-in that day.
- **Sickness.** A sickness report is a fact, not a request. Employees report themselves. Admin and Büro can record one for someone. A correction changes the same report and needs a reason. There is no diagnosis field, by design. Only the person and Admin or Büro see sickness type and evidence status. The shared calendar shows a neutral absence. Active sickness sets the target to 0 and does not block clock-in.
- **Teams.** Teams are date-effective planning shortcuts and grant no rights. Picking a team in an assignment adds the members active on that date.
- **Qualifications.** Admin and Büro maintain a catalog of skills and certifications, assign entries with validity and evidence status and set requirements on jobs. Every assignment checks coverage on the planned date. A gap can be overridden only with a recorded reason. „Intern bestätigt“ is an operational fact, not a legal claim. Employees see their own entries read-only. Expiring certificates appear in `Aufgaben`.
- **Attention.** `Aufgaben` shows the approvals a person can decide now, decision notifications and the person's own requests. A badge never counts an item the viewer cannot act on.
- **Dispatch acknowledgement.** Employees confirm or challenge the current revision of a dispatch. An acknowledgement never stands in for attendance or recorded time.
- **Time facts.** Managers inspect time but never record live time for someone else. A correction of one's own time always needs a second time approver. [Time tracking](time-tracking.md) owns the time rules.
- **Time accounts.** Every in-scope personnel record, with or without a login, has an explicitly opened time account. A missing opening balance, schedule or policy blocks the period close for that person and never counts as zero. Employees see their own account and monthly statements.
- **Access and employment lifecycle.** Admin plans activation, suspends, reactivates or ends organization access, and runs employment transitions. None of these deletes the record, its history or the login. A record without a lifecycle is labeled as not controlled. The owner and the last effective Admin are protected. Suspension and access end stop every read at once, in the app and in the database. Before the start date a future starter reads only their own onboarding, not their `Zeitkonto`. The last holder of a responsibility blocks an employment transition until someone else takes it over.
- **Onboarding.** Organization templates have immutable published versions. A plan created from a template holds editable requirements that reference existing documents, qualifications, conditions, schedules, teams or acknowledgements. Only a requirement marked as blocking access delays activation. Missing configuration never shows as complete.
- **Protected personnel documents.** Personnel files have three classes: standard, Admin-only and health evidence. They sit outside the ordinary library. No responsibility, job assignment or document permission grants access to them. Admin sees every class. Büro manages standard files. The person sees only expressly released versions and can upload requested health evidence. An acknowledgement proves that the person saw one exact version. It is not a signature.
- **Removal and account deletion.** A member can be removed only before any time was recorded, so membership and time history survive. For a member with recorded time, the office ends employment and access through the personnel record. A removal marks the personnel record as exited today in the same step, so a refused removal changes nothing. A member with a future entry date is removed only after that date is corrected. A person deletes their own account only while they hold no membership, suspended or ended included. The database refuses deleting the account of a member with recorded time, and a refused deletion keeps the profile.

### Current limits

- [Calendar and resource planning](calendar-and-resource-planning.md#current-product-baseline) owns capacity conflicts. Employee management shows only absence signals. Minimum staffing, shift rotations and date-specific schedule overrides do not exist.
- Vacation and sickness are the only absence types.
- Time-account adjustments, expiry and payout are manual four-eyes events. WerkFlow applies no automatic cap, expiry, payout or money calculation. Time-account balances carry forward. Vacation carryover does not exist.
- Attention is in-app only. No reminder, escalation, notification preference or external delivery exists.
- A sole admin's own vacation request has no eligible approver until named vacation approvers exist.
- Employees cannot propose corrections to their own master data or conditions. Self-service covers vacation, sickness, acknowledgements, released documents and requested evidence.
- Ownership transfer and emergency owner recovery do not exist.
- A member who joins a further organization of the same owner sees it after the next reload or tab return, not live. A join request records who decided and when, but no reason.
- No compensation, payroll profile, payslip, payroll-provider integration, electronic signature, legal retention or complete organization export exists. The only payroll output is a generic payroll-ready export with employee and code mapping.
