# Commercial and finance

The commercial loop takes an SHK business from a customer request to agreed scope, a correct invoice, collected payment and an understandable job result, using the operational record of customers, jobs, time, documents and material. Every legally or financially consequential action stays explicit, reviewable and audited. Native accounting, payroll and tax filing are separate product decisions.

## Current Product Baseline

WerkFlow has offers with Titel, Positionen and totals, invoice drafts, issued and cancelled invoices, and no order or sent invoice yet. Admin and Büro keep offers, invoices and a catalog of Leistungen, and work with the operational records that the commercial loop will consume.

- **Offers.** Under „Angebote“ → „Angebote“, Admin and Büro list every offer, newest first, search it by number, title and customer, filter it by status and page through it. „Angebot anlegen“ asks for a title and a customer, optionally an Einsatzort and an Ansprechpartner of that customer, and opens the new offer on its own page. A press without title or customer marks both fields and focuses the first; a press repeated after a lost answer opens the first offer. An offer gets a yearly number `ANG-<year>-<n>`, the status „Entwurf“, the Rechnungsempfänger by the default of [Auftraggeber and Rechnungsempfänger](customers-and-crm.md#current-product-baseline) for its customer and Einsatzort, the validity and texts of the company profile, and its creator as the responsible person. Its page shows that header. While the offer is a draft, „Bearbeiten“ changes its title, Einsatzort and Ansprechpartner of its customer, Rechnungsempfänger (any active customer, or „Wie am Einsatzort“, which follows the default again), validity, opening and closing text and the responsible member of the office; the customer stays. A save without title, validity or responsible person marks the fields and focuses the first. A save another session made meanwhile refuses the older form instead of overwriting it; a press repeated after a lost answer gets the first save's result. A kept archived Rechnungsempfänger or a responsible person who left the office does not stop an unrelated change. The create and each change write their history entry, with each changed field's old and new value, in the same database call. An open request with a customer also becomes an offer once, from its conversion dialog ([Customers and CRM](customers-and-crm.md#current-product-baseline)); that offer shows the request it came from. Below the header, a draft's editor holds its content: Titel in order, each with Positionen in order. A Position is typed by hand (Bezeichnung, Beschreibung, quantity with up to three decimals, a unit from the inventory unit list, a net price and a VAT rate of 19, 7 or 0 %; a negative quantity credits), is taken from an article or a Leistung, or is a text line without an amount. „Artikel hinzufügen“ under a Titel adds one unit of the picked article with its name, description, unit, VAT rate and today's net sale price per price quantity, read by the rule of an invoice line from the catalog: an archived article, one without a sale price today and one at another VAT rate are refused with the reason. „Leistung hinzufügen“ adds one unit of the picked Leistung the same way, with the price of today's version. An entry whose stored unit is outside the list leaves the unit empty, and the save asks for one. The Position keeps every value as its own and names its source („Aus Artikel …“, „Aus Leistung …“, „Archiviert“ once the entry is). Another organization's article or Leistung is refused, and an entry a Position names is archived, never deleted. Every priced Position keeps an optional net cost per price quantity as its own value: an article brings its purchase cost of the day, a Leistung its cost rate, and the office can type or change it. A markup in percent on the cost sets the price, rounded once by the line rule; the price is what counts, and a typed price clears the markup, which is not stored. Each Position shows its cost and its margin in euros and in percent of its amount; „Kalkulation“ shows cost, net sale amount and margin per Titel and for the offer, follows every keystroke and names the Positionen without a cost, whose whole amount counts as margin. Cost and margin stay with Admin and Büro: no signed-in role can read a cost, the offer read a document is built from carries none, and only the offer page reads the costs, in one database call with the content. Arrows move a Titel or a Position, and a save numbers them by their order (`1.2`; a text line carries no number). Each Position's amount is quantity × price ÷ price quantity, rounded half away from zero once; a Titel shows its subtotal, and the offer net, the VAT per rate on the sum of that rate's Positionen, and gross, by the same rule as an invoice draft. The amounts follow every keystroke; „Speichern“ sends the whole content, and the server computes and stores every amount again, with each Position's text, unit code and label, quantity, price and rate. A save without a Titel name, Bezeichnung, quantity, unit or price marks the fields and focuses the first. A content save another session made meanwhile refuses the older content; another session's save shows at once while nothing is typed, and with own changes the editor offers „Neu laden“. A header change does not make the content stale, nor the reverse. Each content save writes its history entry with the gross total before and after in the same database call; the content tables have no grant for a signed-in role (`supabase/tests/offers.sql`). The customer's page lists its newest offers and links to all of them. A customer an offer names as customer or Rechnungsempfänger stays from deletion. Field workers see neither the area nor any row.
- **Invoice drafts.** Under „Rechnungen“, Admin and Büro list every invoice, last changed first, search it by number, customer and Rechnungsempfänger, filter it by status and page through it. „Rechnung erstellen“ asks for the customer and optionally one of its jobs, projects or Servicefälle, and opens the new draft on its own page. The customer page (unless the customer is archived) and the job, project (with a customer) and Servicefall pages offer „Rechnung erstellen“ too, with that customer and work filled in. The draft's Rechnungsempfänger is the current one of that work ([Customers and CRM](customers-and-crm.md#current-product-baseline)) when the work's Auftraggeber is the customer, else the customer. On the draft's page the office writes its lines (name, description, a quantity with up to three decimals and negative for a credit, a unit from the inventory unit list, a net price, VAT 19, 7 or 0 %), the Leistungsdatum or Leistungszeitraum, the Zahlungsziel in days and an optional Skonto in percent and days (both start with the organization's defaults), the customer's order number and reference (up to 60 characters each), and an opening and closing text. „Aus dem Katalog“ adds a line from an active Leistung or article: its name, description, unit, VAT rate and today's net sale price per its price quantity, read through the Leistung and article price readers; the office can change every copied value. An archived entry, one without a sale price today, one with a unit outside the list and one with a VAT rate other than 19, 7 or 0 % is refused with its reason. The line keeps the Leistung or article it came from, only of the same organization; the editor names it under the line and marks it „Archiviert“ once that entry is archived. Each line amount and the totals (net, VAT per rate on the sum of that rate's lines, gross) follow every keystroke through the one invoice core; a save computes them again on the server and stores them. A save with a line without name, quantity or price marks the fields and focuses the first. A save from an older version is refused and asks for a reload; another session's save replaces an untouched editor. A draft has no number and can be deleted. Every create, save and delete writes its history entry in the same database call, and a deleted draft's history stays. A customer a draft names as customer or Rechnungsempfänger stays from deletion. Field workers see neither the area nor any row.
- **Issuing.** On a saved draft, „Vorschau“ opens the PDF it would become, marked „Entwurf“, without a number and without e-invoice data. „Ausstellen“ asks for confirmation, then the server builds the invoice from the draft, the company profile and the Rechnungsempfänger's name and address, and checks it with the CEN EN 16931 rules and the renderer under the number and Rechnungsdatum it will take. A draft that fails stays a draft and the page lists what to fix, each with a link to the company details or the customer when one of them is at fault: a missing line or Leistungsdatum, an address without street, postal code and city, a missing tax identifier, a head too tall for the lines. A draft that passes gets, in one database call, exactly that number of the organization's series and that Rechnungsdatum (the Berlin day); if another invoice took the number meanwhile, the server checks it again under the next one. It also gets a copy of seller, letterhead and Rechnungsempfänger, the status „Ausgestellt“, its history entry, and its original: the ZUGFeRD PDF/A-3 with the embedded XML that the server rendered under that number and stored before the call, registered as a document of the customer in the ten-year class dated the Rechnungsdatum and filed. An issued invoice, its lines, number and original never change and are never deleted by any role or path; only the deletion of the whole organization removes them. Its page shows every field read-only and „Original öffnen“, which opens the filed document in the library. If the original could not be stored, the invoice is issued without it, the page says „Beleg ausstehend“ and „Beleg erstellen“ renders the same bytes again; the number stays. A repeated „Ausstellen“ after a lost answer answers with the first number.
- **Storno and correction.** On an issued invoice, „Stornieren“ and „Korrigieren“ ask for a reason; a press without one, or with a control character the invoice XML cannot carry, marks the field. The server checks the Stornorechnung with the EN 16931 rules first, then in one database call issues it with the next number of the same series: a corrected invoice (type 384) that names the cancelled invoice and its Rechnungsdatum (printed above its notes, not in the head; its own number stands in its title, so its head is no taller than the cancelled invoice's), with every line's quantity negated, the cancelled invoice's Leistungszeitraum, references, customer and Rechnungsempfänger as issued, nothing due, and the reason as a note. The cancelled invoice keeps every value and its original, and reads „Storniert“; an invoice is cancelled once, and a Stornorechnung is never cancelled. The Stornorechnung's original is stored before that call and filed in it, like any invoice's; if it could not be stored, the cancel still stands, the Stornorechnung's page says „Beleg ausstehend“, and „Beleg erstellen“ files it later. „Korrigieren“ also opens a new draft with the cancelled invoice's lines and terms, linked to it; its Rechnungsempfänger follows the rule of a new draft, so a recipient corrected on the work or customer applies. Each invoice's page shows the chain of original, Stornorechnung and correction with their numbers and states, the Stornorechnung's page its reason, and the list names the invoice a Stornorechnung or correction refers to; a search by the original's number finds all three. Cancelling is its own explicit step: nothing else cancels an invoice. A repeated press after a lost answer answers with the first Stornorechnung.
- **Invoice settings.** Under „Einstellungen“ → „Rechnungen“, the Admin sets the number series: a prefix (`RE-` to start), the year in the number with a restart at 1 each year (on to start), the least number of digits (3 to start) and the next number, for example to continue the previous software's series. The page shows the number the next invoice takes, or that the series has none left, and then no invoice is issued. A year-based series read before New Year's midnight and saved after it asks for a reload. From the first issued invoice on the series is fixed and only issuing moves it. The same page holds the Zahlungsziel and Skonto a new draft starts with (14 days and no Skonto to start). Büro reads the page; employees see neither the section nor the values.

- **Company profile.** Under „Einstellungen“, „Dokumente & Briefpapier“, Admin and Büro keep one profile per organization: firm with legal form, address, contact, register court and number, management or owner, tax number, USt-IdNr., bank details, a logo, stationery for the first and the following pages with their margins, and the opening text, closing text, validity in days and tax rate without an article that a new offer starts with. Each change records who changed which field, from which value to which. A hint names the details a business letter usually carries that are still missing; nothing waits for them. Employees read no profile field. A concurrent save from an older version is refused and asks for a reload, and so is a save from a page loaded for an organization that is no longer the active one. Replacing or removing a logo or stationery someone else changed meanwhile is refused too. A save, upload or removal repeated after its answer was lost gets its first result instead of a refusal. Stationery margins that leave the offer too little room mark the sides at fault. The organization export carries the profile, its history, and the logo and stationery under `Dateien/Briefpapier`.
- **Offer print.** The same section shows a fixed Musterangebot as the PDF a customer would get, with the profile, a made-up customer and made-up prices. They open or download it. Without stationery the PDF draws a letterhead from logo and details and a footer with the company details; with stationery it draws neither, because the paper carries them. Bedarfs- and Alternativposition amounts stand in parentheses, outside the sum. Employees see neither the section nor the file. The PDF comes from the one renderer of generated documents ([document storage](../technical/document-storage-and-access.md#generated-documents)).
- **Muster-E-Rechnung.** In Settings → Dokumente & Briefpapier, Admin and Büro open a Muster-E-Rechnung as a ZUGFeRD PDF (PDF/A-3b, EN 16931) with the company profile and letterhead; the server checks the embedded XML with the CEN EN 16931 rules first and sends the file only when they pass, otherwise it names the missing company details. Employees get nothing.

- **WerkFlow's own invoices.** When Stripe finalizes an invoice of a customer's WerkFlow subscription, WerkFlow's server reads it back from Stripe and issues its original with Stripe's number and amounts: a ZUGFeRD PDF/A-3 in the EN 16931 profile, WerkFlow as seller from the server configuration, every line exempt under § 19 UStG, paid by SEPA direct debit (mandate reference and Gläubiger-ID) or by card (last four digits, and the amount already paid). The original is issued only when its amounts equal Stripe's, it passes the EN 16931 rules and its embedded XML reads back unchanged; otherwise nothing is issued and Stripe delivers the signal again, for up to three days in live mode. The payment means come from the payment method Stripe debits, not from a charge, so a SEPA invoice issues as its debit starts; when the invoice is paid, the payment signal issues any original still missing. Each Stripe invoice gets one original, kept until the end of the tenth year after its invoice year, also after the customer's organization is deleted. It never changes. A refund in Stripe (a credit note on a paid invoice) cancels the document that stands for the invoice with a Stornorechnung: it carries the credit note's number, names the cancelled document and its date, and negates its lines. A partial refund also issues a new invoice for the part that stays, paid in full, with Stripe's lines and one „Erstattung“ line per refund, numbered `<original number>-KR-<NN>` in the order of the refunds; a later refund cancels that new invoice in turn. Storno and new invoice pass the same checks as an original, are recorded together once per credit note and are kept the same ten years. The customer's billing contact then gets one mail per credit note with the Stornorechnung and any new invoice attached; a refused send is sent again on Stripe's next delivery, and a customer without an address in Stripe raises an alarm instead. When a check fails or cannot run, Stripe's amounts differ, or Stripe's object cannot be issued, an alarm reaches WerkFlow's alarm address once per invoice or credit note and failure. Only the organization's Admin may read its rows, on „Abonnement & Abrechnung“ ([employee management](employee-management.md)). The invoice core takes a credit transfer, a SEPA direct debit or a card as payment means, and an amount already paid; it names a debit or card payment as done only when a paid amount is recorded, and a Storno, a credit or a zero invoice says that nothing is due.

- **WerkFlow's billing mails.** WerkFlow mails its customers itself, from the server, in German with „du“; Stripe sends none. When Stripe announces a SEPA renewal, the customer's billing contact gets a debit notice: amount, debit date, last IBAN digits, mandate reference, WerkFlow's Gläubiger-ID and contact. After the original is issued, the invoice mail carries it as a PDF and names how it is paid: a SEPA debit with Stripe's debit date and the same notice details, or a card. Once Stripe reports the invoice paid, a receipt follows. Each mail goes once per invoice or renewal; a mandate or account changed before the debit gets a new notice. A refused send is sent again on Stripe's next delivery and by the daily comparison. A SEPA debit whose notice left too little lead time ([`invoice-mails.ts`](../../lib/werkflow-billing/invoice-mails.ts)), or that has no debit date from Stripe, raises an alarm to WerkFlow's alarm address. An alarm beside a customer mail is recorded before either is sent and goes after it, so it never holds back the customer's mail or takes its place in the day's quota; one that cannot go out is sent by the daily comparison. A card renewal gets no notice.
- **Daily comparison with Stripe** ([`reconciliation.ts`](../../lib/werkflow-billing/reconciliation.ts) holds its windows). Once a day the server compares Stripe's recent invoices with the issued originals and invoice mails, sends a missing invoice mail and the billing mails and alarms still undelivered again, oldest attempt first, and checks that Stripe's recent signals arrived. Each gap raises one alarm: an invoice without its original or mail, a mail still undelivered, signals gone quiet, or a comparison that did not run the day before. A debit notice whose renewal has passed is not sent; after its alarm it ends as expired. A backlog of undelivered mails longer than one run waits for the next and marks the run incomplete. Every attempt counts, also one that fails before its mail is sent, so each undelivered mail gets its turn.

- **Documents.** Jobs, projects, customers and service cases hold linked documents, and the central library stores business documents. An uploaded PDF is not a structured offer, contract, invoice, incoming bill, payment or accounting record.
- **Invoice recipient.** Each request, job, project and Servicefall keeps its Auftraggeber and its Rechnungsempfänger with the recipient's name and address as they were when set ([Customers and CRM](customers-and-crm.md#current-product-baseline)). Nothing invoices, prices or sends from it.
- **Leistungen.** Under „Angebote“ → „Leistungen“, Admin and Büro keep priced services without an article: number, name, description, kind (Arbeit, Anfahrt, Pauschale, Gebühr, Fremdleistung), a unit from the inventory unit list and a VAT rate of 19, 7 or 0 %. Each price is a version with a first valid day, a net sale price and an optional internal cost rate in cents per unit. A recorded version never changes and is never deleted: a later price starts on its own day, and a second price for the same day replaces the first for every later read while the history keeps both. A Leistung is archived and restored, never deleted. The list shows today's price and the day of a coming one, pages and searches on the server, and its price dialog shows every version. One server reader answers the price of named Leistungen on a day, shaped like the article price reader. Field workers see neither the area nor any row; the database grants the tables to no signed-in role (`supabase/tests/leistung_prices.sql`).
- **Inventory prices.** Articles carry dated purchase and sale prices ([inventory](inventory.md)), a tax rate and billable defaults. One server reader gives Admin and Büro, for a day, an article's base unit, net purchase cost and list sale price in cents per price quantity of base units, each with its first day and source, the later of the two days (the asked day while no price applies), and its tax rate; an archived article answers too, and a field worker is refused. A line amount is quantity × price ÷ price quantity, rounded half away from zero to the cent once per line, in integer arithmetic. A recorded use of job material keeps the purchase price of that moment. A confirmed wholesaler delivery or CSV import changes an own article's prices only by new dated rows after a preview of each change; a wholesaler's list price less the office's rebate per rebate group becomes the net purchase cost, rounded once per price quantity ([inventory](inventory.md#current-product-baseline)). An invoice draft's line from the catalog takes the list sale price of that day. An offer Position takes an article's or a Leistung's sale price and keeps its cost from everyone but Admin and Büro (Offers above); no workflow turns them into revenue or profit.
- **Time.** Time entries attach to operational work. No billable-time handoff, labor calculation, rate card or post-calculation exists. The payroll-ready time export carries minutes only and creates no wage calculation, posting, billable labor or invoice.
- **Measurements and change work.** Aufmaß and Regie- or Änderungsnachweise carry internal decisions and customer-response evidence. They carry no price, scope acceptance, billing release or invoice effect.
- **Handover readiness.** An office-reviewed handover can be released as ready for commercial review, with or without exceptions. The release creates no calculation, price, tax decision, billable quantity, offer, invoice, payment or message.

### Current limits

- An article's purchase price is organization-wide, not per supplier.
- An offer's customer cannot be changed, and an offer has no PDF, sending, acceptance or conversion. A Position's cost is frozen only once sending freezes the content. No Position is a Bedarfs- or Alternativposition or a discount or surcharge in percent of its Titel or the offer (werk-flow/werkflow-planning#382); a fixed discount is a Position with a negative quantity. A hand-typed Position is priced per one unit (one from an article keeps the article's price quantity), and a Position moves only inside its Titel. An article picked under a Titel added since the last save adds no Position when that save shows before the article's price arrives; the office picks it again (werk-flow/werkflow-planning#575). The offers list shows no amount. Its status stays „Entwurf“. Only a request's conversion sets the work an offer was written for; nothing sets a job or project yet.
- An issued invoice is not sent yet: no email. A correction is a Storno and a new invoice: no partial credit note. The correction names the invoice it replaces only in WerkFlow, not in its own e-invoice data. A deleted correction draft is not replaced. An invoice with a negative total is not cancelled, and a draft's Rechnungsempfänger is chosen only through its work or customer. A free line is priced per one unit. A line from the catalog keeps the price it was added with: a later catalog price changes no line. The organization has one number series. No tax case beyond the rates 19, 7 and 0 %.
- The invoice is addressed to the Rechnungsempfänger, who is the EN 16931 buyer; the customer stays linked in WerkFlow and is not printed when they differ. The printed address is the Rechnungsempfänger's address text (street, then postal code and city), also when they differ from the customer; a foreign address cannot be issued.
- A retry of „Beleg erstellen“ renders the same bytes with the same build; a deployment that changed the renderer in between renders other bytes for an original not yet stored.
- No offer uses the profile's tax rate yet; the Musterangebot keeps its fixed 19 %.
- A Leistung's price applies per one unit; a VAT rate other than 19, 7 or 0 % cannot be chosen.
- Not built: calculation, order confirmation, contract, change order, billable measurement, credit, incoming bill, payment, open item, dunning, bank matching, accounting export, ledger.
- A draft offer names its Rechnungsempfänger as a customer and keeps no copy of its name and address; sending will copy them (werk-flow/werkflow-planning#16).
- In WerkFlow's own invoices, a discount, customer balance or tax in Stripe stops the issue. A credit note on an open invoice, or one that carries tax, raises the alarm and issues nothing.
- No XRechnung and no § 13b. PDF/A is checked in CI, not at run time; the CII schema itself is not checked at run time. A stationery PDF prints as the plain letterhead on invoices.
- Issued invoices carry a ZUGFeRD (EN 16931) original; WerkFlow claims no XRechnung, Peppol, DATEV, GAEB, REB/VOB, §13b, GoBD archive, double-entry accounting, payroll or tax-filing capability.
