# Commercial and finance

The commercial loop takes an SHK business from a customer request to agreed scope, a correct invoice, collected payment and an understandable job result, using the operational record of customers, jobs, time, documents and material. Every legally or financially consequential action stays explicit, reviewable and audited. Native accounting, payroll and tax filing are separate product decisions.

## Current Product Baseline

WerkFlow has offers without positions, and no order or invoice yet. Admin and Büro keep offers and a catalog of Leistungen, and work with the operational records that the commercial loop will consume.

- **Offers.** Under „Angebote“ → „Angebote“, Admin and Büro list every offer, newest first, search it by number, title and customer, filter it by status and page through it. „Angebot anlegen“ asks for a title and a customer, optionally an Einsatzort and an Ansprechpartner of that customer, and opens the new offer on its own page. A press without title or customer marks both fields and focuses the first; a press repeated after a lost answer opens the first offer. An offer gets a yearly number `ANG-<year>-<n>`, the status „Entwurf“, the Rechnungsempfänger by the default of [Auftraggeber and Rechnungsempfänger](customers-and-crm.md#current-product-baseline) for its customer and Einsatzort, the validity and texts of the company profile, and its creator as the responsible person. Its page shows that header. The create writes its history entry in the same database call. The customer's page lists its newest offers and links to all of them. A customer an offer names as customer or Rechnungsempfänger stays from deletion. Field workers see neither the area nor any row.

- **Company profile.** Under „Einstellungen“, „Dokumente & Briefpapier“, Admin and Büro keep one profile per organization: firm with legal form, address, contact, register court and number, management or owner, tax number, USt-IdNr., bank details, a logo, stationery for the first and the following pages with their margins, and the opening text, closing text, validity in days and tax rate without an article that a new offer starts with. Each change records who changed which field, from which value to which. A hint names the details a business letter usually carries that are still missing; nothing waits for them. Employees read no profile field. A concurrent save from an older version is refused and asks for a reload, and so is a save from a page loaded for an organization that is no longer the active one. Replacing or removing a logo or stationery someone else changed meanwhile is refused too. A save, upload or removal repeated after its answer was lost gets its first result instead of a refusal. Stationery margins that leave the offer too little room mark the sides at fault. The organization export carries the profile, its history, and the logo and stationery under `Dateien/Briefpapier`.
- **Offer print.** The same section shows a fixed Musterangebot as the PDF a customer would get, with the profile, a made-up customer and made-up prices. They open or download it. Without stationery the PDF draws a letterhead from logo and details and a footer with the company details; with stationery it draws neither, because the paper carries them. Bedarfs- and Alternativposition amounts stand in parentheses, outside the sum. Employees see neither the section nor the file. The PDF comes from the one renderer of generated documents ([document storage](../technical/document-storage-and-access.md#generated-documents)).

- **Documents.** Jobs, projects, customers and service cases hold linked documents, and the central library stores business documents. An uploaded PDF is not a structured offer, contract, invoice, incoming bill, payment or accounting record.
- **Invoice recipient.** Each request, job, project and Servicefall keeps its Auftraggeber and its Rechnungsempfänger with the recipient's name and address as they were when set ([Customers and CRM](customers-and-crm.md#current-product-baseline)). Nothing invoices, prices or sends from it.
- **Leistungen.** Under „Angebote“ → „Leistungen“, Admin and Büro keep priced services without an article: number, name, description, kind (Arbeit, Anfahrt, Pauschale, Gebühr, Fremdleistung), a unit from the inventory unit list and a VAT rate of 19, 7 or 0 %. Each price is a version with a first valid day, a net sale price and an optional internal cost rate in cents per unit. A recorded version never changes and is never deleted: a later price starts on its own day, and a second price for the same day replaces the first for every later read while the history keeps both. A Leistung is archived and restored, never deleted. The list shows today's price and the day of a coming one, pages and searches on the server, and its price dialog shows every version. One server reader answers the price of named Leistungen on a day, shaped like the article price reader. Field workers see neither the area nor any row; the database grants the tables to no signed-in role (`supabase/tests/leistung_prices.sql`).
- **Inventory prices.** Articles carry dated purchase and sale prices ([inventory](inventory.md)), a tax rate and billable defaults. One server reader gives Admin and Büro, for a day, an article's base unit, net purchase cost and list sale price in cents per price quantity of base units, each with its first day and source, the later of the two days (the asked day while no price applies), and its tax rate; an archived article answers too, and a field worker is refused. A line amount is quantity × price ÷ price quantity, rounded half away from zero to the cent once per line, in integer arithmetic. A recorded use of job material keeps the purchase price of that moment. No workflow turns them into offers, invoices, revenue or profit.
- **Time.** Time entries attach to operational work. No billable-time handoff, labor calculation, rate card or post-calculation exists. The payroll-ready time export carries minutes only and creates no wage calculation, posting, billable labor or invoice.
- **Measurements and change work.** Aufmaß and Regie- or Änderungsnachweise carry internal decisions and customer-response evidence. They carry no price, scope acceptance, billing release or invoice effect.
- **Handover readiness.** An office-reviewed handover can be released as ready for commercial review, with or without exceptions. The release creates no calculation, price, tax decision, billable quantity, offer, invoice, payment or message.

### Current limits

- An article's purchase price is organization-wide, not per supplier.
- An offer's header cannot be changed yet, and an offer has no Titel, Positionen or amounts, no PDF, sending, acceptance or conversion. Its status stays „Entwurf“. Nothing sets the job, project or request it was written for yet.
- No offer uses the profile's tax rate yet; the Musterangebot keeps its fixed 19 %.
- A Leistung's price applies per one unit; a VAT rate other than 19, 7 or 0 % cannot be chosen. No offer uses a Leistung yet.
- Not built: calculation, offer positions and amounts, order confirmation, contract, change order, billable measurement, invoice, credit, incoming bill, payment, open item, dunning, bank matching, accounting export, ledger.
- A draft offer names its Rechnungsempfänger as a customer and keeps no copy of its name and address; sending will copy them (werk-flow/werkflow-planning#16).
- WerkFlow claims no XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Peppol, DATEV, GAEB, REB/VOB, §13b, GoBD archive, double-entry accounting, payroll or tax-filing capability.
