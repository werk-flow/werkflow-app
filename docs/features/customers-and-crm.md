# Customers and CRM

The CRM is the operational customer context of an SHK business: who the customer and the right contact are, where the work happens, what was requested and what happened before. It keeps one organization-scoped record from the first request through repeat work and service. It is not a sales CRM with campaigns or deal stages.

## Current Product Baseline

Admin and Büro maintain customers, contacts, work sites, requests, follow-ups and communication guidance. Field workers never open the customer area.

- **Customer list.** Search covers the whole organization, active contacts and sites included, before the list pages. It finds a site by name, street, postal code, city or Objekt number, and a customer by its customer number in the previous system. A customer row then lists the matching sites it is filed under or holds a current or coming role on, each with its current roles and active `Anlagen`; a count names the ones not shown. The list shows active customers; „Archivierte“ shows only the archived ones, with the same search. A page of customers is never a complete selection catalog. A failed read of the list or of a customer section shows the failure with a retry. [Realtime and caching](../technical/realtime-and-caching.md#server-paginated-lists) owns the paging rules.
- **Master data.** A customer is any party that commissions or pays: a private person, a company, a Hausverwaltung, a WEG or an owner, one record however many buildings it relates to. It is private or commercial, with one email, one phone, one free-form main address, notes, an optional manual customer number and an optional customer number from the previous system, each unique in the organization. Creating a customer first compares it with the organization's customers: the same customer number, the phone in any notation or the email in any case on the customer or a contact, the same name with a postal code of the main address or a site, or the same street and postal code. Archived contacts and sites count. The matches show with their reasons and „Öffnen“ in a new tab; while one shows, saving needs a second press, „Trotzdem neu anlegen“. Changing the name, email, phone or address checks again. Archived customers count. Nothing is merged, linked or blocked.
- **Archive and delete.** Archiving keeps a customer with everything that links to it: it leaves the default list and the customer pickers of work (the job list filter keeps it, so its work stays findable), and its detail says since when it is archived and offers „Wiederherstellen“. A picker still shows the archived customer a record already has. Deleting is for a mistaken entry: the server refuses it for every role while a job, project, request, Servicefall, Anlage, maintenance record, document or follow-up links to the customer, and the dialog then names those links and offers archiving instead. An unlinked customer is deleted with its contacts, sites and guidance.
- **Master-data history.** Every change to a customer's name, type, customer numbers, address, email or phone, to a contact's name, email, phone or site role, or to a site's name, address or Objekt number writes a history entry with actor, old and new value in the same database call. Archiving and restoring the customer, a contact or a site write one too, and so do starting and ending a site role, in the chronology of the site's customer and of the holder. The history is append-only.
- **Contacts and sites.** A customer has several `Ansprechpartner` and several `Einsatzorte`. At most one of each is primary, and saving one as primary takes the mark from the previous one in the same save. A site (the Objekt) has a structured address, an optional Objekt number from the previous system, access notes and an optional on-site contact. A contact can belong to one of its customer's sites as `Mieter` or `Zugangskontakt`; it stays a contact, never a customer, and moving out archives it. A field worker's access proposal from a job can correct a Zugangskontakt or add one ([access boundary](#current-product-baseline)). One click adopts the main address as the first site. Archived contacts and sites stay visible and restorable but leave the work pickers. A contact's email is one address or blank; the save refuses anything else and marks the field. Only a stored email that is one address becomes a mail link. An older value that is not shows as text, and editing that contact asks for a valid address or an empty field before it saves.
- **Site roles.** A site carries dated roles `Eigentümer`, `Hausverwaltung` and `Rechnungsempfänger`, each pointing at an active customer of the organization, the site's own customer included. A start date may stay unknown. One role of a kind holds a site at a time. „Wechseln“ ends the current holder the day before the new one starts, in the same database call; „Beenden“ sets the last day. An ended role stays as history, also when its holder is archived. Each site shows its current roles, or that none is recorded, and its Mieter and Zugangskontakte. A customer holding roles on other customers' sites lists them under „Rollen an Einsatzorten“, ended ones under „Früher“. A role between two customers keeps both from deletion. The `Anlage` stays at the site.
- **Work references.** A job references one site and one contact of its customer. A project's default site and contact prefill its jobs, and each job can override them. When the customer has no site, the job form and the conversion offer „Einsatzort anlegen“, which saves the first site to the customer and selects it. Selecting a site copies its address into the job as a snapshot, so a later site edit never rewrites where work happened. Changing the customer of a job or project clears the old site and contact. The assigned field worker sees the site, its access notes and a click-to-call contact.
- **Requests.** An `Anfrage` is fast to capture during a call. Only the summary is required. An unknown caller is free text. A call ends with one accountable next step: „Rückruf bis“, set by a quick choice or an exact time, and its owner, prefilled with the capturing user. Once a site is chosen, its active `Anlagen` are offered. The saved Anlage belongs to the request's organization, customer and site. A new customer or site without a named Anlage drops it. Urgency „Notfall“ shows a fixed notice to follow the company's own emergency procedure. WerkFlow gives no technical advice. It is later matched to a customer or promoted into a new one without retyping. An unmatched request lists the organization's customers the caller matches by the same rules and reasons as a new customer, the captured address giving the postal code and street, each with one-press „Zuordnen“. While one matches, promotion needs a second press. Promotion creates the customer, its primary `Ansprechpartner` from the caller's name, phone and email, its first `Einsatzort` from the captured address, links all three and writes the request history in one step, or changes nothing. The conversion then starts with that site and contact, and „Ort“ with the site's address, else the captured one. Capture, edit, close and reopen each save the request and its history entry in one step. A press repeated after a lost answer, within one opening of the capture dialog or before the promotion succeeded, returns the request or customer the first press saved and writes nothing again. The captured caller data stays on the request.
- **Request lifecycle.** A request is open, optionally in clarification, then converted, added to existing work, or closed with a required reason. Managers can reopen a closed request. A converted or added request is final and read-only. Every change is in the request history. Open requests appear in `Aufgaben` with owner and age. An open request whose promised callback has passed shows „Rückruf überfällig“ in the list and the detail. In `Aufgaben` it is its owner's task. While it has no owner who is still a manager, every manager sees it.
- **Once-only conversion.** A request converts exactly once, deliberately, into a new job, a new project or a reactive service case. Conversion needs a resolved customer. It creates the work, marks the request converted, links the attachments as a second link to the same file and writes the history in one step, or changes nothing. Request and work link to each other. A service case also links the request's Anlage. Nothing is scheduled, assigned or sent. A press repeated after a lost answer within one opening of the conversion dialog returns the work the first press created, without a second history entry. Any other second conversion, one to another target included, is refused before it creates any work.
- **Adding to existing work.** An open request with a customer can be added once to an open job, project or service case of that customer instead of becoming new work. The dialog lists the customer's open work, the request's site first, and narrows it by number, title or site. One step writes the request's summary and details into the work's „Hinzugefügte Anfragen“, links its attachments as a second link to the same file, marks the request „Hinzugefügt“ with a link to the work, and writes the request history, or changes nothing. Managers see the added requests on the work's detail page, each with its date, who added it and a link to the request. Work of another customer, closed work and a second addition are refused. A press repeated after a lost answer within one opening of the dialog returns the first result. Work that took a request is not deleted.
- **Chronology.** The customer detail derives a chronology from the customer's records, requests, work, documents, follow-ups, preference history and master-data history. Nothing is copied into a separate timeline. Each entry shows who acted and links to its source.
- **Follow-ups.** A follow-up has one customer, a manager owner, an exact due time and a state: open, completed or cancelled. Follow-ups due soon appear in `Aufgaben`, not in a second inbox. A follow-up whose owner left or lost the role surfaces to all managers.
- **Communication guidance.** Per customer, with contact overrides, each channel is allowed, disallowed or unknown, separately for service, marketing and required commercial purposes. Missing configuration shows as unknown. Calling or emailing the wrong person, over a disallowed channel or against a do-not-contact instruction needs a recorded reason. The guidance sends nothing and draws no legal conclusion.
- **Installed equipment.** Each site lists its recorded `Anlagen` and links to the service-owned detail. An empty list means only that nothing was recorded. CRM never edits the equipment lifecycle.
- **Access boundary.** Customer, contact, site, follow-up and preference records are manager-only. An assignment gives a field worker the contact, site and linked equipment of that job, never the customer record. From a job assigned to them, a field worker proposes a corrected access contact (name, phone, a short note) and access notes for the job's site; the server refuses another job, another site and another organization before it writes. The proposal changes nothing at the site. A job has one open proposal; a second replaces it. Managers decide it in `Aufgaben` under „Zugang prüfen“. Accepting writes the site in one step: the contact corrects the chosen active Zugangskontakt, whose history names the manager, or becomes a new one, and the access notes replace the site's. Rejecting needs a reason. A decided proposal keeps its decision, reason, proposer, decider and times but never the proposed values: an accepted value lives on in the site, a rejected one is not kept. The job shows the worker the proposal's state and the reason; only the proposer sees what was proposed.
- **Work in customer context.** The customer detail lists the customer's jobs and projects. Managers create work there with the customer locked. A project has one customer, and its jobs inherit it. A marked placeholder holds the place of a later financial summary.

### Current limits

- Not modeled: billing recipients on requests, jobs and service cases, address purposes beyond main address and sites, households, contacts shared across customers. The Rechnungsempfänger of a site prefills nothing yet.
- A site stays filed under the customer it was created for; a change of Hausverwaltung or owner changes its roles, never that customer. Field workers see neither the roles nor the Mieter and Zugangskontakte of a site. An accepted access proposal reaches the job's field view through the access notes; its Zugangskontakt shows there only once the office makes it the job's contact. The chronology lists no access proposal.
- Customer numbers are manual.
- The master-data history covers identity fields, site roles and archiving. Notes, a contact's free-text role, a site's access notes and the primary marks keep no history. A write outside the server actions records no actor; the chronology shows „Nicht erfasst“.
- An archived customer's detail page still creates work in its context. The document link picker hides archived customers like the work pickers; the job list filter still offers them.
- Not built: configurable response targets, callback reminders by email or push, merge, alias history, a standing duplicate list, import-quality workflow, customer portal, automated follow-up.
- The chronology records no standalone call, email, message, letter or meeting, and never claims that a manual note was delivered.
- A customer commitment on a planned visit is a calendar fact. It proves no delivery or consent and sends nothing. When the internal plan moves, the office sees a mismatch.
- Preference guidance is conservative. It holds no legal basis, evidence file or retention rule.
- Deletion is for unlinked mistaken entries and archiving keeps the rest. Retention periods, data-subject erasure and legally constrained deletion do not exist.
