# Document management

Document management (`Dokumente`) is the one digital place for job photos, contracts, invoices, offers, reports and general business files, in place of paper folders. Office users get a practical library. Field workers get a simple upload, view and download flow on their jobs. [Document storage and access](../technical/document-storage-and-access.md) owns storage, upload, access and audit mechanics.

## Current Product Baseline

Admin and Büro organize ordinary organization files in the central library. Operational records show their documents in a contextual section. Field workers upload, view and download ordinary files on assigned jobs. Protected personnel files follow separate access rules.

- **Central library.** Admin and Büro browse a manual folder tree, an overview grouped by linked target, a list of all files, views per category and per kind of linked record, and a view of unorganized files, with search, filters, sorting and a separate `Papierkorb`. They create, rename, move, copy and delete folders. They upload single files, batches or whole folders by drag and drop. Batch move, copy and delete work on a multi-selection, and dragging selected files onto a folder moves them. Deleting or copying a folder covers its whole tree or nothing.
- **Paged lists.** Search, sorting and filters cover every authorized document before the library pages its results. Paging clears the selection, so a batch action acts only on the displayed page. A search or filter change made while the library loads keeps its request and its skeleton, even when it lands in the moment an earlier result shows. [Realtime and caching](../technical/realtime-and-caching.md#server-paginated-lists) owns the paging rules.
- **One file, many links.** A document exists once. Links connect it to jobs, projects, customers, employees, requests, installed equipment, service cases and maintenance coverage. A link never copies the file. Converting a request links each attachment to the created work as well. WerkFlow creates no folder when an operational record is created, by decision. Manual folders and link filters organize the library.
- **Contextual sections.** Every linked record shows its documents. Managers attach existing library files on every linked record except a request, manage links in one dialog, and remove a link without deleting the file. A request's documents come only from upload on the request. Assigned employees upload, view and download only on their own job, from the field work pack. On the page of a project with at least one of their jobs, they also view and download the project's own documents, read-only. Job documents there stay limited to their own jobs. A document they read lists only its links to their own jobs, those jobs' projects and customers, and themselves. Employees never see the library, the trash, versions or audit history.
- **Recovery and history.** Delete moves a file to the `Papierkorb`, where managers restore or permanently delete it. A permanent deletion removes the document from WerkFlow at once; the stored file stays long enough for a database restore to find it ([storage cleanup](../technical/document-storage-and-access.md#storage-cleanup)). Contracts, invoices, offers and reports keep numbered versions. Earlier versions can be downloaded, not rolled back. Managers see the audit history of every document. A file, folder, version or audit entry names who uploaded, created or changed it; a person who has left the organization appears by name only, without their email or profile picture. Each upload, new version, link change, rename, category change, move, copy, delete, restore, permanent deletion, retention change and filing saves together with its audit entry, or not at all. An upload registers the file and its link together, and a refused upload keeps no stored file. WerkFlow infers a category at upload, and managers can change it. A category is a label, not a structured record.
- **Retention and filing.** Every document has a document date (`Belegdatum`), by default its upload day. An uploaded invoice, offer or contract gets the ten-year retention class; it is kept until the end of the tenth year after its document date. Admin and Büro change date and class in the details and file (`Festschreiben`) a document there; filing is final. A document date lies between 1900 and one year after today; „Speichern“ refuses any other, such as a year left half-typed. „Festschreiben“ files nothing while a typed date is not saved or reset, and marks the date. A filed document takes no new version and never goes to the `Papierkorb`, but keeps rename, move, category and links. No document is deleted permanently, and no organization is deleted, while a retention period runs. The library and the details show the filed state and the retention end. Employees see none of it. Protected personnel documents keep their own rules.
- **Interrupted uploads.** The upload dialog lists each file of a library upload, a contextual upload and a new version until the server registered it. A file whose transfer or registration failed stays listed with its reason, and one retry sends only the failed files. A retry never uploads an arrived file twice: when the earlier try's bytes reached storage, it registers those first. A new-version retry whose earlier answer got lost recognizes the version its own upload saved and reports it saved. A version another upload saved meanwhile, even under the same file name, is a conflict: the retry says a newer version was saved, offers no retry and reloads the versions. While files are on their way, closing the dialog warns that unfinished files are lost, and the browser asks before the page is left. Only a registered file shows as saved, also after a reload.
- **Viewer.** PDFs and images open in a large in-app viewer with a download fallback. A link can open one exact document. The customer chronology uses such links.
- **Evidence and handover.** A work-template item may name an expected evidence category without creating a file. A document can be tied to one exact work-artifact revision as evidence, closure proof, signature mark or export. An ordinary upload never becomes evidence by itself. A handover release freezes exact document versions and stores one customer-safe package as an ordinary document.
- **Protected personnel documents.** A personnel file is a separate access class outside the ordinary library. It belongs to the personnel record, not to an employee link. The affected employee reaches only expressly released versions. A release grants reading, never a change. No job assignment or document permission widens this access. [Employee management](employee-management.md) owns the access classes.
- **History guards.** Linking a document to equipment writes an equipment-history event. From then on, WerkFlow refuses to unlink that document from the equipment or to delete it permanently. Only removing the equipment releases the link. Equipment, service-case and coverage links give an assigned employee no document access beyond the assigned job.

### Current limits

- Not built: OCR, invoice parsing, AI classification, thumbnails, dedicated offer, contract or invoice records.
- A filed document is never deleted on its own, also after its retention period; it goes only with its organization. Nothing deletes a document when its period ends.
- No external delivery, public link or customer portal exists for any document or handover package.
