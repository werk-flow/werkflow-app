# Service and maintenance

Service and maintenance covers installed customer equipment (`Anlage`), reactive service (`Servicefall`) and recurring maintenance (`Wartungsplan`). It is a distinct SHK operating model, not another job status. It keeps track of the equipment at each site and its history, plans recurring maintenance reliably, and dispatches reactive work with enough context.

## Current Product Baseline

Admin and Büro manage installed equipment, reactive service cases and maintenance plans. Service builds on the customer, job, planning, dispatch, template, evidence, time, document and inventory owners and copies none of their facts. A service slice never creates a parallel customer, job, time, document or inventory system.

- **Installed equipment.** Managers register equipment on one `Einsatzort` with a free position label, and one component level below a root system. Buildings, apartments and technical rooms are not separate levels. Equipment has a stable number, identifiers such as serial numbers, warranty and commissioning facts, document and work links, a lifecycle state and a searchable history that nobody can edit. Unknown technical facts stay visibly unknown. Replacement creates a successor and keeps the predecessor. The replace dialog lists the predecessor's running plans with their next due date and its components, each carried to the successor by default; a plan left on the predecessor needs a reason. An Anlage with more running plans than one replacement carries cannot be replaced in one step: the dialog says so and the save is refused. The successor shows the predecessors' history after its own, marked „beim Vorgänger“ and paged on the server. Correcting a replacement carries the successor's plans and components back before it voids the successor. Removal or decommissioning keeps past service records. Equipment can carry its „Nummer im Altsystem“, the number the previous program gave it: optional, not unique, set in the create and edit dialogs, shown on the detail when set, and found by the Anlagen list search in any case. A replacement does not take the predecessor's old number. Equipment has no contact of its own. The site contact applies. Customer pages show a compact site view.
- **Installed equipment is not inventory.** Inventory tracks what the trade business owns or stocks. Installed equipment is what sits at a customer site and gets maintained.
- **Reactive service.** Managers create a case directly or qualify an existing `Anfrage` exactly once, with the Anlage the request named. The case keeps the customer's original statement and is triaged against one site and exact equipment, with urgency, access guidance, and a suspected warranty, contract, goodwill, rework or charge context. That context is operational triage only. Duplicate, related and continuation links keep both cases. WerkFlow never merges or deletes a duplicate. A case connects to one job, which runs through the normal calendar, dispatch, field-work, evidence and follow-up flows. The case detail shows its Auftraggeber and frozen Rechnungsempfänger with the site and its access contacts ([Customers and CRM](customers-and-crm.md#current-product-baseline) owns the rules). Triage, commercial context and the recipient stay office-only.
- **Maintenance plans.** A versioned plan binds one site, exact equipment and one published work-template version. It creates due work for an 18-month horizon before any job exists. Its first due date lies at most five years back. Creating, revising or activating an active plan generates its due work in the same save, so an active plan never stands without its due work. Overlapping active coverage needs an explicit reason. A plan carried to a replacement's successor gets a new revision bound to the successor, and every open or visit-created due item bound to the predecessor, also one an earlier revision left open, moves to it with its due date unchanged; completed due work stays with the predecessor. Two open items for the same date become one, the other superseded with a reason. An outstanding item whose revision names other Anlagen beside the predecessor than the current revision refuses the replacement, so no item silently changes the Anlagen it covers. The new revision continues the current revision's cadence after its latest due date, so a later generation neither recreates settled due work nor skips a date. Coverage dates and renewal signals are entered operational facts, not legal or commercial contract truth. No plan is inferred from existing equipment, jobs, requests, documents or warranty dates.
- **Maintenance visits.** A manager deliberately creates a visit job for a due item, then schedules it in a separate step as a normal appointment. The visit job takes its number and the plan's template and attaches to the due work in one step, so a refused attachment leaves no job and no used number. Scheduling creates the appointment and attaches it to the due work together. A stale due item or an appointment for another job refuses both. Compatible due items may share one visit. Moving an appointment is a calendar fact and never rewrites the plan or other due work. Editing one appointment, one future appointment or the maintenance definition has clearly different effects. Completion links the exact submitted evidence revision, records a separate outcome (erledigt, teilweise erledigt or nicht erledigt), advances the next due date, and may link a service case or create a follow-up. A partial or unresolved visit names one to ten open Mängel and its next step in the same save: Rückkehr, Garantieprüfung or Austausch anbieten, each with an owner (an Admin or Büro member, by default the completing manager) and a target date after the visit, or Keine with a reason. Each step but Keine gives its owner a customer follow-up on the visit job, due on the target date. A Rückkehr also opens a return due item on the same plan revision, so on the same equipment, due on the target date, which lies before the regular next due date. The return item never moves the regular next due date, and completing it does not advance the plan. The finding, the follow-up and the return item keep their history and are never rewritten. A complete visit names no Mängel and no next step. The Mängel stay open until a later visit of the same Anlage, or of its Nachfolger, is completed with any outcome for the same or a later day. That completion closes every earlier open Mangel in the same save and records which visit closed it and who completed it; an unfinished visit names what is still open. A missed or cancelled visit closes nothing. The Anlage's page shows „Offene Mängel“ with the visit, plan, visit job, next step, owner and target date while any are open. A visit that did not happen never drops its due item. A manager records „Besuch hat nicht stattgefunden“ with a reason (Kunde nicht angetroffen, abgesagt, verschoben, or another reason with a note): the due item returns to open with its due date, without the job and the appointment, and its history names the missed job and the reason. The job and its appointment stay as they are. Cancelling a visit job reopens each of its due items without an outcome the same way, in the same save. A completed item is never reopened. The office then creates a new visit for the open item.
- **Servicebericht.** „Abgeschlossene Wartungsbesuche“ on the Wartung page lists each completed visit with a job, newest first, paged on the server. „Servicebericht erstellen“ renders a PDF for the customer: the company letterhead, the job's Rechnungsempfänger as recipient, the Einsatzort, the visit date, each Anlage, the performed work and measurements of the visit's customer-facing evidence, the outcome, the open Mängel, the next step in customer words with its target date, and the customer's signature with name and day. It never prints internal evidence, the completion reason, the next step's owner or reason, triage, coverage, prices or costs. The report is filed at once as a document of the job, each Anlage and the customer, without a retention class. „Neu erstellen“ files a further report beside the earlier one; a report someone else made meanwhile refuses it. „Herunterladen“ fetches the filed PDF; the assigned technician finds it among the job's documents. Admin and Büro only.
- **Ownership split.** Planning owns appointment time, series edits, capacity and skipped or cancelled appointments. Service owns maintenance plans, coverage and due-work identity. Site evidence supplies the reusable artifacts that the Servicebericht and the history print. Service makes no legal or price decision, sends no message, changes no stock, splits no time and encodes no manufacturer rules.
- **Dispatch.** Open requests and customer follow-ups appear in `Aufgaben`, including the next step of a partial or unresolved maintenance visit for its owner, named with the plan and the visit job. Cases awaiting a visit and open due work appear in the Servicefälle list and the Wartung workspace, not in `Aufgaben`. Dispatch shows qualified available employees, site and access context, travel feasibility from entered facts, material demand marked as not reserved and tools marked as not assessed. Reassignment replaces the dispatch revision. An employee can challenge an assignment, which a manager may keep with a reason. Each recipient's acknowledgement is visible.
- **Lists.** The Anlagen and Servicefälle lists search, filter, count and page on the server. The Wartung workspace pages its open due work, plans and coverages on the server: one search covers all three, each list pages on its own, and each tab count covers every matching record in the organization. Due work is überfällig while it is open or has a visit job without an outcome and its due date lies before today in Berlin. The due list sorts by due date, so overdue work comes first, marks each overdue row, and offers the filter „Überfällig“ with the count of matching overdue items. A failed read on a service page shows the failure with a retry. A plan on a deactivated site stays listed with its due work.
- **Bereitschaft.** Admin and Büro plan and hand over on-call shifts under „Service → Bereitschaft“, and every role sees who is on call on the dashboard. [Calendar and resource planning](calendar-and-resource-planning.md#on-call-coverage-bereitschaft) owns the rules.
- **Field work pack.** The assigned technician gets one work pack on the job: customer, site, contact, call and navigation, access notes, ordered instructions with expected evidence, measurements, defects, photos, notes, customer signature on an exact evidence revision, time and travel through the shared clock, and planned material with take and return. The pack shows only the equipment linked to the job, the issue and access context of a linked case, and the plan, equipment and instructions of a maintenance visit. Each linked Anlage opens „Verlauf und Dokumente“ online: its completed maintenance visits with date, outcome, the defects and measurements of their customer-facing evidence and the visit's open Mängel, and each replacement, including the Vorgänger's, newest first, ten at a time with „Mehr laden“; and its ordinary documents, such as a Bedienungsanleitung. A financial paper, an office-only document and internal evidence stay closed. Coverage dates, renewal risk and internal notes are manager-only. The technician reports a blocker. The office owns follow-ups, equipment history and the final report.

### Current limits

- The field history shows maintenance visits and replacements only: no Servicefall visit, and nothing of it offline.
- A completion closes every earlier open Mangel of the Anlage; it cannot keep one open except by naming it again.
- WerkFlow does not send the Servicebericht; the office downloads it and sends it itself. It prints the signer's name, not the drawn signature.
- Not built: phone forwarding for Bereitschaft, customer messaging, telemetry, automated diagnosis, offline or native mobile use.
