# Problem reports

A member reports a problem from the app to WerkFlow support. The report stays in WerkFlow's private EU storage, only its reporter and WerkFlow support read it, and it is deleted on the owner's periods.

## Current Product Baseline

Every member reports a problem from any page and follows their own reports in „Meine Meldungen“. WerkFlow support triages every report in one queue and files reports for callers. The server keeps a scrubbed copy of each report; report threads can receive that copy and the redrawn screenshot, and answer. No original and no attachment leaves WerkFlow. The hand-out stays off until the owner sets its secret.

- **Button.** „Problem melden“ sits on every page of the app: in the phone header and at the foot of the sidebar, which the phone menu shows too. On the desktop, F2 opens it as well, unless another dialog is open. It never covers the clock dock.
- **Capture.** Until the press, the open tab keeps in memory only the route templates and labels of the last pressed controls and the names and codes of client errors since load; nothing is stored or sent. At the press it captures the page's route template, that list, the app version, the device and browser family, the window size and the offline state. A control's label enters the report only when it is one of the app's own UI strings; a route enters only as its template, such as `/auftraege/[jobNumber]`. The server refuses a context of any other shape.
- **Screenshot.** The press takes the [redrawn screenshot](../technical/problem-report-scrubbing.md#screenshot-redraw) before the dialog opens. The dialog shows it; the person draws boxes over anything they want to hide, there or in a large view that „Vergrößern“ opens over the dialog, and removes the last box or all of them. The boxes are burned into the image as an opaque striped cover before it leaves the device. „Screenshot mitsenden“ is on by default; off, the report is text only. A device that cannot redraw sends no screenshot, never a plain one.
- **Dialog.** A description up to 5,000 characters is required; on a phone the keyboard's own dictation key fills it, with no audio stored and no speech service. Up to five photos or PDFs go along as attachments. „Senden“ shows its progress at once. The report saves first, once, under an id the device chose for it, so a retry after a lost answer finds it; then its files go through a signed upload; when a file fails, the report stays saved, the dialog names what is missing, and „Senden“ sends only that. A screenshot or attachment whose bytes already left the device no longer changes, and a retry registers those bytes.
- **Receipt.** After sending, a banner names the report's number, which counts from 1 in each organization and never changes, and leads to „Meine Meldungen“.
- **Meine Meldungen** (`/meldungen`, through the account menu): every role sees only the reports they sent in the active organization, newest first, with number, sending time, status and support's answer. It reads through the server on each open, and again when support changes a status or an answer or files a report for the person: that change touches a signal row which only the reporter may read and which holds no report content, and the open list reads again.

- **Support.** WerkFlow support is the persons whose Auth user ids the server setting `SUPPORT_USER_IDS` lists ([environments](../technical/environments.md#credentials)), one list per environment. Support is no organization role and needs no membership. Every support action admits only a verified user of the list, and a missing, empty or malformed setting refuses every support operation. Everyone else gets the not-found page for every address under `/support`, an `admin` of the reporting organization included.
- **Queue** (`/support`). Every organization's reports, oldest first, with filters for the status, open by default, and for the bucket. A row names the organization and shows the scrubbed copy; while a report has none, it shows in words that the text is still being scrubbed, could not be scrubbed, or is gone, and no text.
- **Triage.** On a report's page, support sets the status, the bucket, the assignee and the answer to the reporter. An assignee is a support person of the list with an account, named from their profile. A save sends only the changed fields with the values the page showed for them, and one database call writes them with one history entry each; the answer's text stays out of the history. A field someone else changed since is a conflict: the save is refused, the page shows what is saved under the field and keeps the input, and the next press saves over it. Closing sets the closing time and reopening clears it, so the deletion periods count from the last closing. The other support persons' open queue and report pages read again on their own signal row, which holds no report content; a triage, an opening of an original and a report filed for a caller move it.
- **Original.** A report's page shows the scrubbed copy, its scrubbed context and the redrawn screenshot. „Original öffnen“ shows the original description, the reporter's name, the captured context and the attachment list; an attachment downloads through a short-lived signed address. The server writes each opening into the history before the original leaves it, and hands out nothing when that entry fails. A report whose originals are deleted says so and gets no entry.
- **Filed for a caller** (`/support/aufnehmen`). Support picks the organization and one of its members, writes down what the caller said and adds attachments. The member is the reporter and finds the report in „Meine Meldungen“; a member chosen under one organization never stays chosen under another, and after a filing the form starts empty; the support person is the filer, and only they add its files. The database refuses a person who is no member of the organization. Such a report has no captured context and no screenshot, and the server scrubs it after the save as it does a member's report.

- **Hand-out to report threads.** Report threads outside WerkFlow pull the [scrubbed copy](../technical/problem-report-scrubbing.md#hand-out) through four route handlers under `/api/problem-reports/v1/reports`. Each one admits only the bearer secret `PROBLEM_REPORT_HANDOUT_SECRET` ([environments](../technical/environments.md#credentials)), never a user session. Without the secret every handler answers 503 and reads nothing; a missing or wrong token answers 401. Every answer is `no-store`, and logs carry codes only.
  - **Waiting** (`GET …/reports`): open reports, sent at least ten minutes ago so their screenshot is uploaded, with a scrubbed copy of the current rules that no thread received yet, oldest first: each report's id and bucket, nothing else, so a dispatcher starts a waiting blocker first.
  - **Hand-out** (`POST …/reports/{id}/hand-out`): the report's id, scrubbed description, scrubbed context, status, bucket, whether it has an answer (never support's text) and the address of its screenshot on this API. One database call claims the report and writes the hand-out into the history before the copy leaves, so a report goes to one thread once and then leaves the waiting list. A report without a scrubbed copy of the current [scrub rules](../technical/problem-report-scrubbing.md#hand-out), or sent less than ten minutes ago, is refused as `copy_pending`, or as `copy_unavailable` when no such copy can come; a closed one as `report_closed`, a second hand-out as `already_handed_out`. An unknown id answers like a malformed one.
  - **Screenshot** (`POST …/reports/{id}/screenshot`): the redrawn screenshot of a report already handed out, as bytes through the server, each time with a history entry first.
  - **Write-back** (`POST …/reports/{id}/write-back`): bucket, status and the answer to the reporter of a report already handed out, at least one of them, each with the value it was based on. A thread that never wrote the answer can only expect none. When support changed one of those fields since, nothing is written and the answer is `triage_conflict` with the status, bucket and answer flag now. It goes through support's triage call without an actor: the history names „Agent“, closing and reopening work as for support, and the reporter's and support's pages move live. The thread sets no assignee and clears no field.
  - **Automatic answer.** An answer the Agent wrote carries „Automatisch geschrieben“ in „Meine Meldungen“ and on support's report page, until support writes the next one.

- **Scrubbed copy.** After the receipt, the server writes the [scrubbed copy](../technical/problem-report-scrubbing.md#text-scrubbing) of the description and the context: the organization's known names, addresses and phone numbers, every email address, phone number and IBAN, and a name after a salutation become placeholders such as `[Kunde 1]`. A control label stays only when it is one of the app's own UI strings, and a token of the capture that is a known name or number is removed. The daily run scrubs each report that still has no copy.

- **Report.** A report belongs to an organization and its reporter. It holds the original description, up to 5,000 characters, and the captured context, a JSON object. It names who filed it, which differs from the reporter only when support files it for a caller.
- **States.** A report starts as `eingegangen`, then moves through `in_bearbeitung` to `erledigt` or `abgelehnt`; a closed report carries its closing time. Its bucket is `blocker`, `fehler`, `unklarheit` or `wunsch`. The scrubbed copy is `ausstehend` until the scrubber writes it, then `bereinigt`. It is `nicht_bereinigbar` when a scrub failed; then no copy exists until a later scrub succeeds. A later scrub needs the originals: when the deletion run removes them first, the report keeps no text at all.
- **Files.** A report keeps at most one screenshot beside its attachments, and takes no file once its originals are deleted. [Report files](../technical/document-storage-and-access.md#report-files) owns the storage rules.
- **No customer reads a report.** The report tables are server-only: no `admin`, `buero` or `employee` reads one through the Data API, and the organization export leaves them out. A member submits only into an organization they belong to, and only the person who filed a report adds files to it.
- **History.** A report's history holds status, bucket, assignee and response changes, every opening of an original by support and every hand-out to a report thread, with actor and time. A report thread's entries carry no account and read as „Agent“. It refuses changes and deletion, goes only with its report, and keeps the actor's id after that account is deleted.
- **Fixed identity.** A report's organization, reporter, number and sending time never change, and its originals change only through the deletion run.
- **Deletion periods** (owner's answer on werk-flow/werkflow-planning#21). The originals (description, context, screenshot, attachments) are deleted 90 days after the report closes, at the latest 12 months after it was sent. The scrubbed copy is deleted 24 months after the report closes, with the report, its status and its history. A daily run, `/api/problem-report-retention` on production, applies both, one database call per report; the deleted files reach the [storage purge](../technical/document-storage-and-access.md#storage-cleanup). Its answer and logs carry counts only. An account deletion takes the reports the person reported along, an organization deletion all of its reports.
