# temporary-transcripts

Subtitle transcripts of YouTube/Instagram videos the product owner found valuable — currently mostly UI/UX advice. They exist as **inspiration and nudges, never as requirements**: nothing in here is a rule, a backlog, or something that "has to" be implemented. Each idea must be weighed individually against WerkFlow's actual context (German SHK businesses, calm operational UI, the design language in `.claude/skills/werkflow-design`) and adopted only where it genuinely fits; most won't apply, and that's expected.

Ground rules for agents:

- Read transcripts when a task explicitly points here (e.g. the UI/UX consolidation); don't treat them as standing required reading.
- Anything adopted gets documented through the normal channels (design skill, feature docs, decision records) on its own merits — never cite "the video said so" as rationale, and never reference this folder from durable docs.
- Transcripts are raw auto-generated subtitles: expect transcription errors, filler, and platform-specific advice that doesn't transfer.

This folder is deliberately undocumented elsewhere and will likely be deleted in the future.
