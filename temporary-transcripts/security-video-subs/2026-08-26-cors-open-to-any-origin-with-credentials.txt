## Caption

Your API is configured to accept requests from any origin with credentials. 

That means any website can make authenticated requests as your users. The browser sends cookies automatically. The attacker reads everything. Hardcode an origin allowlist. Lock down session cookies. Restrict methods and headers per endpoint. 

Your users trust your domain. Your server is handing that trust to anyone who asks.
