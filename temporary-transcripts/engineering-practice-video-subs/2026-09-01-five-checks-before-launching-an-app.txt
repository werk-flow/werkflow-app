## Caption

These 5 things have cost people real money, and all easily avoidable.

**1. Terms and conditions that actually cover you.**

UGC, arbitration, data ownership, privacy. Most founders copy-paste a template from a competitor and hope. That template was written for a different business model than yours.

**2. Run a read/write load test against your primary data object.**

Not the whole app. The one table everything touches. That’s where it falls over, and it falls over on your best day, not your worst.

**3. Do a real security audit.**

I use a Claude skill for this now. Takes an hour. Finds the exposed keys, the missing auth checks, the endpoint you forgot was public.

**4. Test your backup by restoring it somewhere.**

A backup you’ve never restored is not a backup. It’s a folder.

**5. Test live payments in production.**

Real card. Real money. Refund it after. Sandbox lies.

None of this is sexy.

But getting the app to work is the easy part. The stuff that kills software companies happens after you hit deploy.

Save this for the week before your launch.
