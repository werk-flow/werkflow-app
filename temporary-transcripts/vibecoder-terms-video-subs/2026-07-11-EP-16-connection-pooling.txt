So what is connection pooling and why is it important?
So connection pooling keeps a small number of active connections to your database
and spreads all of your incoming requests to the database among those connections.
The alternative to this is opening up a connection every time a request comes in and then closing it after.
So in effect, your app borrows a connection, uses it and then releases it to the next user.
The logic here is that database connections are expensive to spin up and the database can only support so many.
