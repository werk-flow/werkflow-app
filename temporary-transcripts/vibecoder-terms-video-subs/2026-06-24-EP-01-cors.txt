Welcome to day one of teaching technical terms to non-technical vibe coders because nobody else will.
Today's term is CORS. This stands for Cross Origin Resource Sharing. You can pretty much think of it
like the guest list at a party. It's the backend server saying which sites are allowed to read its
responses. So these are the steps. It goes from server to browser and finally to your site. So
if your front-end requests are getting rejected by your backend's CORS policy, this is always going
to be a backend code change fix. What you need to do is add your front-end domain to the backend
server's CORS policy. Knowing technical terms helps you write better prompts, so follow for more.
